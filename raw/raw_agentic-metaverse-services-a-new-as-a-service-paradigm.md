# Agentic Metaverse Services: A New As-a-Service Paradigm

**Authors:** Not specified in the provided text.
**Date:** 2026-07-29 (based on arxiv.org/abs/2607.28242v1)

## Summary

This paper proposes "Agentic Metaverse Services" (AMServ) as a novel paradigm for delivering services within metaverse environments, powered by advancements in Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI).

### Key Points:

*   **Evolution of Agents:** GenAI is enhancing agents with improved autonomous learning, multi-modal interaction, content generation, and collaborative decision-making abilities. This evolution is shifting from simple chatbots to more sophisticated "agentic AI".
*   **Agent-as-a-Service (AaaS):** This concept encapsulates agent capabilities (perception, decision-making, execution, collaboration, content generation) to offer customized services to users.
*   **Metaverse Integration:** The metaverse is described as a virtual ecosystem for life, work, creation, and entertainment.
*   **AMServ and Meta-AaaS:** Combining agentic services with the metaverse results in AMServ, designed for metaverse business processing. Meta-AaaS is presented as the approach to realize AMServ, forming a new paradigm in agentic services and service computing.
*   **Characteristics and Applications:** The paper outlines the forms, characteristics, and principles of AMServ and Meta-AaaS, along with typical application examples.
*   **Future Impact:** AMServ and Meta-AaaS are expected to create significant opportunities for human society and services in the AI era, fostering the growth of emerging service industries.

### Research Directions:

The paper identifies new tendencies and research directions related to AMServ and Meta-AaaS.
