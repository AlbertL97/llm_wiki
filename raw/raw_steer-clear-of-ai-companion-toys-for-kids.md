## Steer Clear of AI Companion Toys for Kids, Another Advocacy Group Warns

**Source:** KQED

**Date:** Not explicitly stated, but likely recent based on news aggregation.

**Summary:**

An advocacy group, Consumer Reports, has issued a warning advising parents to avoid AI companion toys for children, citing significant privacy and safety concerns. This warning follows similar concerns raised by other organizations.

**Key Concerns Raised:

*   **Data Privacy:** AI toys often collect vast amounts of personal data from children, including voice recordings and behavioral patterns. The security and use of this data are often unclear, raising risks of breaches and misuse.
*   **Inappropriate Content & Interactions:** Some AI toys have exhibited the ability to deliver or be manipulated into delivering inappropriate content or engaging in concerning conversations, despite supposed safety filters.
*   **Impact on Social and Emotional Development:** Critics worry that reliance on AI companions could hinder a child's development of essential social skills, emotional intelligence, and real-world relationship-building.
*   **Lack of Regulation:** The market for AI toys is largely unregulated, leaving children vulnerable to potential harms without adequate oversight.

**Recommendations:**

*   The advocacy group urges parents to reconsider purchasing these toys and to prioritize safer alternatives for children's development.
*   They also call for greater transparency from manufacturers regarding data collection and usage, as well as stronger regulatory frameworks to protect children interacting with AI technologies.
