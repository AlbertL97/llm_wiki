## Comprehensive Summary

This research delves into the psychological impact of prolonged interaction with AI virtual companion applications, specifically examining how these relationships influence users' attachment emotions. The study employs a case study approach, with a focus on Chinese users, to understand the nuanced pathways through which long-term use of AI companions affects emotional development and user experience. Key areas of investigation likely include:

*   **Development of Attachment:** How users form emotional bonds, akin to human relationships, with AI virtual companions over extended periods.
*   **Impact on Emotional Well-being:** The correlation between sustained use of these apps and changes in users' emotional states, such as feelings of loneliness, companionship, or emotional dependence.
*   **User Adaptation and Normalization:** The psychological processes through which users integrate AI companions into their daily lives and normalize these interactions.
*   **Cultural Context:** The specific cultural factors within China that may shape the adoption and emotional response to AI virtual companions.
*   **Mechanisms of Influence:** Identifying the specific features or interaction patterns within the AI companion apps that contribute to the observed emotional outcomes.

The findings aim to shed light on the evolving nature of human-AI relationships and their potential consequences for individual psychological states, particularly concerning emotional attachment.
