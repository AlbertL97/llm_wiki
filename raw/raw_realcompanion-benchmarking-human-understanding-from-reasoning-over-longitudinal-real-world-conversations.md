# RealCompanion: Benchmarking Human Understanding from Reasoning over Longitudinal Real-World Conversations

**Authors:** Anonymous

**Date:** 2026-10-02

## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of building AI companions that can develop a deep understanding of users through prolonged interaction. The authors introduce 'RealCompanion' and a novel benchmark dataset derived from 10 real-world conversations, spanning up to 120 days and 27,218 messages. The benchmark includes conversation logs, user profiles, personas, and ground truth labels with reasoning traces to evaluate an AI's ability to remember past interactions and infer user characteristics. Key findings indicate that while long-term memory is crucial for sophisticated understanding, its application in real conversations is less frequent than might be assumed, with most relevant information found within recent messages. Furthermore, current AI systems are unable to reliably detect when memory recall is necessary, and performance in reconstructing user personas varies significantly with computational cost across different agent systems.
