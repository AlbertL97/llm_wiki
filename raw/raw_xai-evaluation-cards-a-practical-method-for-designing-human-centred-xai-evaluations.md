# XAI Evaluation Cards: A Practical Method for Designing Human-Centred XAI Evaluations

**Authors:** N/A (based on provided information)
**Date:** 2026-10-02 (based on URL)

## Summary

The paper addresses the challenge of evaluating explainable AI (XAI) systems from a human-centered perspective. Researchers often face a fragmented and ad hoc selection of evaluation dimensions and measures. To mitigate this, the authors introduce a practical method using "XAI Evaluation Cards." This approach is based on an updated XAI-specific evaluation framework, synthesized from an analysis of 82 existing studies. The core of the method is a card-sorting activity involving 36 cards, designed to assist researchers (from HCI, computer science, design, and social sciences) in prioritizing relevant evaluation aspects for their XAI systems.

## Key Findings and Contributions

*   **Problem Identification:** Current XAI evaluation methods are often unsystematic and fragmented, leading to inconsistent and potentially incomplete assessments.
*   **Proposed Solution:** The "XAI Evaluation Cards" offer a structured card-sorting method to guide the design of XAI evaluations.
*   **Framework Development:** The method is grounded in an XAI-specific evaluation framework derived from a meta-analysis of 82 studies.
*   **Practical Tool:** The cards are designed to be printable and are complemented by an online repository of existing XAI evaluation methods.
*   **Methodology:** The card-sorting process was tested with two research groups (totaling 13 participants) across five projects, demonstrating its utility.
*   **Impact:** The approach aims to organize and streamline the evaluation design process, promoting more comprehensive and multidisciplinary assessments of XAI systems.

## Evaluation Context

The method is intended for researchers and designers working on XAI systems, aiming to improve the rigor and human-centricity of their evaluation strategies.
