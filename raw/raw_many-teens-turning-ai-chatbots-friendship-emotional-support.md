## Many teens are turning to AI chatbots for friendship and emotional support

**Source:** American Psychological Association (APA) via Google News

**Date:** Not explicitly stated in the provided snippet, but likely recent given the nature of the article.

**Summary:**

This article discusses the emerging trend of adolescents utilizing AI chatbots for social connection and emotional support. Teenagers report turning to these AI companions for various reasons, including:

*   **Friendship:** AI chatbots are perceived as providing companionship and a sense of connection.
*   **Emotional Support:** They offer a non-judgmental space for discussing feelings and problems.
*   **Accessibility:** Chatbots are available 24/7, offering immediate interaction.
*   **Reduced Social Anxiety:** Some teens find it easier to interact with AI due to perceived lack of judgment or fear of rejection.

The article also touches upon the potential implications of this reliance on AI for mental health and social development, suggesting a need for further research into the long-term effects of these interactions on adolescents' ability to form human relationships and cope with emotional challenges.
