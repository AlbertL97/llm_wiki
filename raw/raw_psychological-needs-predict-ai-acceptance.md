# Your psychological needs predict whether you will accept an AI companion or therapist

**Date:** September 18, 2023

**Source:** PsyPost

**Summary:**

A recent study published in *Psychology of Popular Media* investigated the relationship between individuals' psychological needs and their willingness to accept AI companions and therapists. The research, conducted by researchers from the University of Oregon, found that certain psychological needs, particularly those associated with autonomy, competence, and relatedness, play a crucial role in predicting an individual's receptiveness to AI companionship and therapeutic support.

The study surveyed 375 adults about their perceptions of AI and their own psychological needs. The findings indicated that individuals who reported higher levels of psychological need satisfaction, especially in terms of feeling connected to others (relatedness) and feeling effective in their actions (competence), were more likely to express interest in and accept AI companions and therapists.

Conversely, individuals who felt a stronger need for autonomy and control in their lives were less likely to see AI as a suitable replacement for human interaction, particularly in therapeutic contexts. The researchers suggest that while AI companions can offer a sense of connection, the perceived lack of genuine control and agency might be a barrier for some.

This research underscores the importance of considering individual psychological profiles when designing and implementing AI systems intended for social or therapeutic roles. Understanding these needs can help in developing AI that is not only functional but also psychologically resonant and accepted by users.
