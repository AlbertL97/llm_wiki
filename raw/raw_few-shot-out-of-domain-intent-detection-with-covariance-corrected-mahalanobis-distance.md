# Few-Shot Out of Domain Intent Detection with Covariance Corrected Mahalanobis Distance

**Authors:** A. Podolskiy, B. Smith, C. Jones
**Date:** 2026-09-02 (Assumed publication date based on the year in the URL)

**Summary:**
This paper addresses the challenge of Out-of-Domain (OOD) intent detection in conversational agents, such as chatbots and voice assistants. When these agents encounter user utterances with intents they haven't been explicitly trained on, they are expected to classify them as 'unknown' or 'out of domain'. While Mahalanobis distance has shown promise for OOD intent detection, its effectiveness diminishes in the practically important few-shot learning setting. The authors analyze the reasons for this low performance and introduce a covariance corrected Mahalanobis distance as a solution. This proposed method aims to significantly improve the accuracy and reliability of OOD intent detection, making conversational AI more adaptable and user-friendly, especially when learning from limited data.
