## Entropy-Centric Explainable AI for Remote Sensing Image Segmentation

### Summary

Artificial intelligence (AI) models, particularly deep neural networks, are increasingly used in complex problem-solving but often suffer from a "black-box" nature, hindering transparency and trust, especially in critical applications like remote sensing image analysis. This ambiguity limits the adoption of these powerful AI tools. Explainable AI (XAI) aims to address this by making AI decision-making processes understandable. While progress has been made in explaining image classification, image segmentation remains an area with significant room for improvement.

This paper introduces a novel entropy-centric XAI method specifically designed for semantic segmentation tasks. The proposed method aims to provide clearer insights into how and why AI models make segmentation decisions. Furthermore, the paper presents a new methodology for evaluating XAI, designed to efficiently measure the relevance of the highlighted regions produced by the XAI method.

Experimental results indicate that the proposed entropy-centric XAI method outperforms recently adapted XAI methods for semantic segmentation, suggesting its potential to enhance transparency and build trust in AI systems for such applications.
