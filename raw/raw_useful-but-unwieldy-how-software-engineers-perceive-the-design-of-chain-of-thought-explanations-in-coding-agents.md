# Useful but Unwieldy: How Software Engineers Perceive the Design of Chain-of-Thought Explanations in Coding Agents

**Authors:** Unknown
**Date:** 2026-11-24 (based on provided URL structure)

## Summary

Coding agents (CAs) powered by Large Language Models (LLMs) are becoming integral to software engineering (SE) workflows. Understanding the CA's decision-making process through Chain-of-Thought (CoT) explanations is crucial for software engineers to build trust and effectively utilize these tools. This research explores software engineers' experiences with CoT messages as an Explainable AI (XAI) mechanism through a survey and semi-structured interviews with 36 participants.

## Key Findings

*   **Indispensable Tool for Trust and Transparency:** CoT messages were identified as essential for software engineers in gaining transparency, enabling scrutiny of the CA's actions, and fostering trust in the agent.
*   **Challenges and Comprehension Difficulties:** Despite their importance, CoT messages often present usability challenges:
    *   **Over-verbosity:** Explanations can be excessively long, making them difficult to process.
    *   **Mismatched Expertise:** The technical details within CoT messages may not align with the software engineer's specific knowledge level.
    *   **Perceived Detachment:** CoT messages can feel like the agent is communicating with itself rather than directly with the user, leading to a sense of conversational disconnect.
*   **Impact on Usage:** These difficulties can result in comprehension issues, leading to selective use of the CoT feature or complete abandonment of the tool.
*   **Design Implications:** The insights gathered are synthesized to inform the design of more effective CoT messages that better meet the explainability needs of software engineers.

## Methodology

*   Survey
*   Semi-structured interviews
*   Thematic analysis of survey responses and interview transcripts
*   Participants: 36 software engineers

## Contribution

This study provides valuable insights into the practical usability of CoT explanations in the context of human-AI interaction within software engineering, highlighting specific design considerations for improving the effectiveness of XAI in this domain.
