Title: PersuaRL: Reinforcement Learning-Driven Multi-Expert Selection for Persuasive Dialogue Generation in Insurance

Description:
Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly used in digital communication, powering conversational agents for customer service, sales, and insurance. While capable of factual communication, these agents often struggle with persuasive, context-sensitive dialogue, particularly in sensitive areas like insurance where trust and clarity are paramount.

This work addresses this gap by focusing on improving the persuasiveness of digital agents in the insurance domain. Key contributions include:

1.  **InsureDial Dataset:** A new dataset specifically designed to capture the nuances of persuasive communication in motor insurance interactions.
2.  **PersuaRL Framework:** A reinforcement learning (RL)-based framework that enables LLM-driven dialogue agents to adaptively explore, select, and coordinate strategies from multiple expert modules. This coordination is guided by the evolving dialogue context to achieve more effective persuasion.

Evaluations:
Extensive automatic and human evaluations were conducted on two benchmark persuasion dialogue datasets, including the new InsureDial dataset. The results consistently show that PersuaRL outperforms baseline methods in generating contextually appropriate and highly persuasive responses.

Key Findings:
- LLM-driven dialogue agents can be enhanced in persuasive capabilities through specialized frameworks.
- Reinforcement learning is effective for adaptively selecting and coordinating persuasive strategies.
- A dedicated dataset (InsureDial) is crucial for training and evaluating persuasive dialogue in specific domains like insurance.
- PersuaRL demonstrates superior performance in generating persuasive and contextually relevant responses compared to existing methods.
