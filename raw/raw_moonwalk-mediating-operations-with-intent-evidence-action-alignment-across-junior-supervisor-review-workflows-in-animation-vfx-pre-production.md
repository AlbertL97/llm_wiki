# MOONWALK: Mediating Operations with Intent-Evidence-Action Alignment Across Junior-Supervisor Review Workflows in Animation/VFX Pre-Production

**Authors:** N. Ben-Youssef, L. N. B. de Moura, C. A. F. de Almeida, A. S. P. Silva, V. L. P. de Siqueira, V. E. B. de Almeida, A. M. M. de Arruda
**Date:** 2026-09-10

## Description

Animation and VFX pre-production review workflows often suffer from a lack of clarity and traceability as creative intent is translated from senior to junior artists. This can lead to criteria drift, loss of evidential basis for judgments, and poor survival of reasoning through the handoff process.

To address these issues, the authors propose a design framework for **intent-evidence-action alignment**. This framework aims to:

1.  **Articulate Intent:** Define creative intent within a shared project record.
2.  **Anchor Judgments:** Ground review judgments to concrete evidence.
3.  **Convert Decisions:** Transform authorized decisions into clear revision tasks linked directly to reference notes.

This framework is instantiated in **MOONWALK**, a professional pre-production review system. MOONWALK includes:

*   A shared intent record.
*   Reference and specification anchoring mechanisms.
*   Tools for structured work-in-progress comparison.
*   A supervisor-authorized action planning module.

Within this workflow, AI primarily handles administrative coordination, such as flagging missing context and organizing notes, while artists maintain full creative control.

## Study and Findings

An in-studio study compared MOONWALK with a **chat-only (chatbot) interface** using matched production materials. Participants' existing workflows served as a retrospective baseline.

The results indicate that MOONWALK led to:

*   Stronger **intent alignment**.
*   Improved **decision traceability**.
*   Enhanced **checklist executability**.

Crucially, the study also confirmed that **aesthetic authority and final prioritization must remain with human practitioners**.

The evaluation concludes that the integrated structured workflow provided by MOONWALK offers significant value over unstructured conversational AI chatbots for this domain.
