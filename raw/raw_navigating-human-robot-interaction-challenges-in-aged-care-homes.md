## Navigating Human-Robot Interaction Challenges in Private and Residential Aged Care Homes

This chapter delves into the deployment of robotic systems and ambient intelligence within aged care environments, specifically residential aged care (RAC) homes and private dwellings for older adults.

**Key Challenges and Context:**
*   **Extreme and Challenging Environments:** Both RAC homes and private residences are characterized by cluttered and dynamic layouts, diverse resident needs, and limited on-site technical support.

**Types of Systems Explored:**
*   **Embodied and Non-Embodied Systems:** These assist with daily living activities, safety monitoring, and social engagement.
*   **Hybrid Deployments:** Integration of robots with fixed and wearable sensors.

**Design Considerations for Older Adults:**
*   **Cognitive, Physical, and Social Needs:** The design must cater to the specific requirements of older adults.
*   **Accessibility:** Prioritizing users with low digital literacy.
    *   Robust yet simple voice interfaces.
    *   Limited sets of non-verbal cues.
    *   Accessible screen-based companions.
    *   Safe object handover mechanisms.

**Addressing Privacy, Trust, and Acceptability:**
*   **Practical Strategies:**
    *   On-device inference to minimize data transmission.
    *   Data minimization principles.
    *   Visible consent controls for users.
    *   Transparent data handling practices, aligned with AI and privacy regulations.

**Evaluation Metrics:**
*   **Real-World Focus:** Emphasis on practical, operational metrics.
    *   False alarms per day.
    *   Time to recover from system faults.
    *   Staff workload reduction.
    *   Long-term system reliability.

**Future Directions:**
*   **Open Research Challenges:** Identifying areas for further development.
*   **Scalable and Regulation-Ready Deployments:** Aiming for widespread adoption that adheres to legal and ethical standards.
*   **Support for Aging:** Ensuring dignity, safety, and independence for older adults through technology.
