# Worth the Wait? How Response Time and Streaming Shape Judgments of Generative AI Answers and Chatbot Engagement

## Key Findings:

*   **Response Time and Delivery Format Impact Perception:** The speed and method of delivery for generative AI chatbot answers significantly influence user judgments and engagement.
*   **Streaming Preferred Over Instant Replay:** Answers that are streamed (unfold as they are written) are judged more positively than identical answers presented all at once (replayed).
    *   In blind preference votes, a streamed answer won significantly more often than a replayed version of the same answer.
    *   Users perceived replayed answers as less complete, more superficial, and more often incorrect compared to streamed answers.
*   **Slower Responses Can Deter Follow-up Engagement:** While streamed answers might not get penalized in direct preference votes compared to faster, replayed answers, delays caused by congestion can reduce user likelihood of asking follow-up questions.
    *   Slower first exchanges led to a reduced probability of users asking a follow-up question.
    *   In large-scale ChatGPT use, replies slowed by congestion made users less likely to continue the conversation.
*   **Implications for AI Service Design:** The findings have implications for how AI services cache and stream answers, how they budget latency, and how preference leaderboards evaluate AI models.
*   **Counter-Intuitive Results:** The results suggest that faster is not always better and that the delivery mechanism plays a crucial role in user experience, contrary to some design expectations.
