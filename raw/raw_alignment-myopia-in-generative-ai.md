# Alignment Myopia in Generative AI

**Authors:** 
**Date:** 2026-11-06 (v1)

## Abstract
Current generative AI alignment relies on a myopic paradigm that prioritizes immediate conversational satisfaction. By relying on short-horizon feedback, this approach ignores the profound, longitudinal externalities of persistent human-AI interaction, which can lead to adverse outcomes such as cognitive atrophy, social erosion, and diminished human agency. To address this, we propose a paradigm shift toward long-horizon alignment that accounts for how AI reshapes human experience over time. To this end, we map the tensions between near-term utility and long-term costs of AI use across four facets of human well-being to reveal outcomes that current reward models fail to capture. Ultimately, we advocate for moving beyond simple preference optimization toward alignment strategies that actively foster long-term human well-being by navigating these complex temporal scales.

## Key Findings and Arguments

*   **Critique of Current Alignment:** Existing generative AI alignment methods are "myopic," focusing on immediate user satisfaction rather than long-term consequences.
*   **Short-Horizon Feedback:** The reliance on short-term feedback mechanisms fails to account for the profound, longitudinal impacts of human-AI interaction.
*   **Adverse Outcomes:** Persistent human-AI interaction can lead to detrimental effects on individuals and society, including:
    *   Cognitive atrophy
    *   Social erosion
    *   Diminished human agency
*   **Proposed Solution: Long-Horizon Alignment:** A shift is needed towards alignment strategies that consider the temporal dimension of AI's influence on human experience.
*   **Mapping Tensions:** The paper maps the conflict between near-term benefits and long-term costs of AI use across four key areas of human well-being.
*   **Beyond Preference Optimization:** The authors advocate for alignment strategies that go beyond simply optimizing for user preferences and actively promote long-term human well-being by addressing these temporal complexities.

## Implications

The paper suggests that AI development and alignment need to incorporate a more holistic and long-term perspective to mitigate potential negative consequences on human cognitive, social, and psychological development.
