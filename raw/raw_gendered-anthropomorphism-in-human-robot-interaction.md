## Abstract

This research explores the phenomenon of gendered anthropomorphism in human-robot interaction (HRI). It specifically investigates the impact of robot gender on human motivation when individuals collaborate with robots on tasks. The study aims to understand whether assigning a specific gender to a robot influences user engagement, perceived helpfulness, and overall motivation to complete tasks, thereby contributing to the broader understanding of how social cues and human biases are projected onto artificial agents.
