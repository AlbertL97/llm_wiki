**Title:** Unboxing Diffusion Models for the Arts: Interactive Model Bending and Practice-Based Explainability

**Abstract:**
Explainable AI (XAI) in creative practice can be less about technocentric explanation and more about enabling artists to inspect modify and debug models as part of making. Yet largescale texttoimage diffusion systems are typically presented as opaque endtoend tools limiting this kind of material engagement. We argue that even large models can function as creative materials when their internal structure is made visible and manipulable. To support this we propose a handson approach to explainability centred on experimentation and intervention. We instantiate this approach with a model bending and an interactive (inspection) interface integrated into ComfyUIs nodebased workflow including interactive layer selection and intervention controls. Through qualitative and quantitative analysis of bending interventions in Stable Diffusion 15 we show how manipulating specific components of a diffusion pipeline produces relatively consistent families of visual effects allowing artists to build practical layerlevel intuition about how different parts of the model shape generated images.

**Key Findings and Contributions:**
*   **Critique of Traditional XAI:** The paper posits that traditional technocentric XAI is insufficient for creative domains. Instead, XAI should focus on enabling artists to directly inspect, modify, and debug models during their creative process.
*   **Model Bending as Practice-Based XAI:** A novel approach called "model bending" is introduced, which treats AI models as tangible creative materials. This involves making the internal structure of diffusion models visible and manipulable.
*   **Interactive Interface for Diffusion Models:** The authors propose and instantiate a hands-on approach to explainability through an interactive interface integrated into ComfyUI's node-based workflow. This interface allows for layer selection and intervention controls, facilitating experimentation.
*   **Gaining Intuition through Intervention:** Through qualitative and quantitative analysis of "bending interventions" in Stable Diffusion 1.5, the research demonstrates that manipulating specific components of the diffusion pipeline leads to consistent visual effects. This allows artists to develop practical, layer-level intuition about how different model parts shape image generation.
*   **Democratizing Understanding of Large Models:** By making large diffusion models more accessible and understandable through interactive manipulation, the approach aims to empower artists and creators.
