# Deepak Chopra's new AI companion puts decades of wisdom 'in your pocket,' he says

**Source:** CNBC

**Date:** October 26, 2023

**Summary:**

Deepak Chopra, a well-known figure in spiritual and wellness circles, has introduced a new AI companion named "DeAI." This AI is developed to encapsulate Chopra's extensive knowledge and teachings, making them accessible to users on demand. Chopra emphasizes that this AI is not just a tool but a companion that can offer guidance and wisdom, drawing from his lifetime of work in areas such as mindfulness, meditation, and personal transformation.

Key aspects highlighted:

*   **Accessibility of Wisdom:** The AI is presented as a way to carry "decades of wisdom 'in your pocket,'" suggesting convenience and constant availability of Chopra's insights.
*   **Focus on Well-being:** The companion aims to assist users in their mental well-being journey, offering guidance on stress reduction, emotional balance, and personal growth.
*   **Conversational Interface:** Users can interact with the AI through natural language, allowing for a more personalized and engaging experience.
*   **Potential for Companionship:** While not explicitly a social robot, the AI's role as a "companion" suggests potential for emotional support and a sense of connection, blurring lines between a tool and a confidant.
*   **Ethical Considerations (Implied):** The development and deployment of such an AI raise questions about the nature of advice, the authenticity of AI-driven wisdom, and the potential impact on human relationships and mental health practices.

Chopra's venture into AI companionship signifies a growing trend of integrating AI into personal lives for self-help and guidance, particularly in the realm of mental and spiritual well-being.
