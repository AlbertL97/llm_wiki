Summary:
The article "The Labor of Storytelling: How People with Chronic Illnesses Leverage AI and Communities for Narrative Translation" investigates the evolving role of AI, particularly large language models, in helping individuals with chronic illnesses translate their lived experiences into narratives. These experiences, often communicated through bodily symptoms, require social articulation and shape how suffering is understood and addressed.

Key Findings:
- **AI as a Mediator:** LLMs are emerging as new mediators in the landscape of illness storytelling, a role historically filled by communities.
- **Narrative Translation:** AI participates in "narrative translation," transforming illness experiences for specific audiences and purposes.
- **Complementary Roles of AI and Communities:** Participants envisioned distinct yet complementary roles for AI and their existing support communities.
- **Trust in AI:** Trust in AI was influenced by participants' learning about the model's boundaries and their perception of the AI's understanding.
- **Informative Design:** The findings aim to inform the design of future AI-mediated narrative support systems.

Methodology:
The research involved 21 prototype-driven semi-structured interviews with people managing chronic illnesses.
