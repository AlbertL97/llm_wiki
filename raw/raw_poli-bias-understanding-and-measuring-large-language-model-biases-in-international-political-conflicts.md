# Poli-Bias: Understanding and Measuring Large Language Model Biases in International Political Conflicts

**Authors:** Unknown (arXiv preprint)
**Date:** 2026-08-08

## Summary:

Measuring political bias in large language models (LLMs) is complex due to its subtle manifestations in framing, argumentation, and legal reasoning, which are difficult to quantify with single metrics.

This work introduces **Poli-Bias**, a novel counterfactual framework designed to systematically measure whether LLMs exhibit differential treatment of legally equivalent international conflict scenarios based on the countries involved.

### Key Features of Poli-Bias:

*   **Counterfactual Prompting:** Compares LLM responses to paired prompts where country identities are systematically swapped across various geopolitical relationships, legal violations, and reasoning tasks.
*   **Decomposition of Bias:** Moves beyond single-metric assessments by decomposing response disparities into five interpretable dimensions, detailing where and how unequal treatment occurs.

### Findings:

*   The study evaluated 13 contemporary LLMs from diverse model families and sizes.
*   Results indicate that country identities and user affiliations significantly influence how LLMs describe, evaluate, and defend equivalent actions under international law.
*   Poli-Bias demonstrates its effectiveness as a fine-grained framework for auditing the political even-handedness and potential sycophancy of LLMs.
