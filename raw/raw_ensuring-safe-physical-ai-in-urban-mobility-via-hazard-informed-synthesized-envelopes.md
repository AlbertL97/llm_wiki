## Summary

The deployment of heterogeneous robotic systems in urban environments presents significant challenges for maintaining safety, particularly in the context of complex human-robot interactions. This research introduces a unified framework designed to enhance the safety of physical AI in urban mobility.

### Key Findings and Contributions:

*   **Hazard Analysis and Runtime Enforcement:** The framework bridges systematic hazard analysis with runtime enforcement mechanisms through the use of "hazard-informed safety envelopes."
*   **Cross-Layer Safety Transformation:** Safety is not treated as a static constraint within individual software modules. Instead, a process of cross-layer safety transformation is introduced, spanning symbolic, spatial, and dynamic world models.
*   **Unified Representation:** This multi-modal representation of safety naturally interfaces with physical AI runtime harnesses.
*   **Guaranteed Safe Urban Mobility:** The ultimate goal is to guarantee safe operation for AI systems engaged in urban mobility tasks, implying a focus on preventing accidents and ensuring predictable behavior in human-dominated environments.

### Significance:

This work addresses a critical aspect of deploying AI and robotics in public spaces, where safe physical interaction with humans is paramount. By providing a systematic approach to safety that accounts for complex environmental factors and runtime dynamics, it contributes to the reliable and secure integration of robots into urban infrastructure.
