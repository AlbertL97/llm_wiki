## Designing Human-mediated AI Guidance: Ready Together for Personalized Family Emergency Preparedness

**Authors:** Not specified in the provided text.
**Date:** 2026-08-21 (based on URL date)

**Summary:**
This research focuses on the emerging area of human-mediated AI guidance, where a human acts as an intermediary to adapt and deliver AI-generated information to a recipient. The paper introduces a framework for this type of interaction and applies it to 'Ready Together,' an AI system designed to assist families in preparing for emergencies. 

Key findings and contributions:

*   **Human-Mediated AI Guidance Framework:** The paper proposes a new framework that acknowledges the necessity of human interpretation and adaptation of AI outputs in certain contexts, especially when the recipient has specific needs or emotional states that an AI might not fully grasp.
*   **Ready Together System:** This system exemplifies the framework by using AI to generate personalized emergency preparedness guidance for families. Parents are positioned as mediators, translating and delivering this information to their children.
*   **Challenges in Family Emergency Preparedness:** Qualitative research (interviews and co-design) identified specific difficulties families face, including reluctance to discuss emergencies with children, uncertainty about how to explain complex information appropriately, and a preference for interactive, family-centered learning.
*   **Design Implications:** The identified challenges directly informed the design of the 'Ready Together' prototype, emphasizing interactive activities and personalized recommendations.
*   **Positive Reception:** Pilot study and heuristic evaluation indicated positive participant responses to the personalized recommendations and practical activities offered by the system.
*   **Preservation of Responsibility:** Preliminary findings suggest that human-mediated AI guidance can effectively support preparedness while ensuring parents retain control over interpreting and communicating information, thus preserving their role and responsibility.

**Contextual Relevance:**
This work is highly relevant to human-AI interaction by proposing a novel interaction paradigm. It touches upon aspects of trust and persuasion as parents decide how to relay AI information. While not directly about mental health interventions, family preparedness can indirectly impact mental well-being by reducing anxiety related to emergencies. The interaction design might also implicitly involve elements of anthropomorphism if the AI is perceived as a helpful assistant.
