## Diffusion TV: Experiencing Diffusion Models through Tangible, Embodied Interaction

**Authors:** Unknown (based on provided arXiv link format)
**Date:** 2026-09-05 (v1 submission date implied by URL)

### Summary Details:

Diffusion TV is presented as an interactive AI art installation designed to provide a tangible and embodied experience of diffusion models. The core of the installation is a modified CRT television. Users interact with the system by physically manipulating the TV's antenna, which in turn controls the clarity of AI-generated images and sounds. This physical act is framed as a metaphorical enactment of the 'denoising' process fundamental to diffusion-based generative models.

The interaction is further contextualized by three distinct channels accessible via a tuning knob. These channels feature AI-generated animals categorized by temporal and ecological themes: Past (extinct species), Present (endangered species), and Future (speculative creatures). This setup imbues the experience with a narrative layer that touches upon temporal and ecological concerns.

The installation emphasizes continuous audiovisual feedback and direct physical interaction. This approach aims to foreground the generative process itself, allowing participants to engage with and understand intermediate states of AI output as experiential material, rather than focusing solely on the final product.

Diffusion TV intentionally avoids explicit technical explanations. Instead, it offers an alternative mode of explainable AI (XAI) that is embodied and exploratory. The goal is to invite users to engage with and reflect upon generative technologies through direct, hands-on experience.
