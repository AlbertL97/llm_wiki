## Abstract

Language models provide a plug-and-play interface between humans and robots, but important challenges remain when speech, dialogue, fast interaction, and collaboration are required. We propose a benchmark for the community to use as a way to explore common spoken dialogue artifacts between robots and humans, including requests for clarification, interruptions, embodied signals (e.g., head nods or facial cues), and time constraints. We also explain our vision to extend the benchmark for other aspects of human-robot interaction that are important to the larger research community. To facilitate the benchmark, we further propose using \textit{Retico}, a real-time communication framework that fulfills important technical requirements to enable robots to have spoken dialogue capabilities.

## Key Findings and Contributions

*   **Problem Identification:** Existing language models and interfaces face challenges when applied to human-robot spoken dialogue, particularly in areas requiring real-time interaction, collaboration, and nuanced communication.
*   **Proposed Solution: Extensible Benchmark:** The authors propose a novel benchmark designed to evaluate and improve spoken dialogue systems for social robots. This benchmark focuses on common dialogue artifacts such as clarification requests, interruptions, and the integration of embodied signals (e.g., non-verbal cues).
*   **Focus on Interaction Dynamics:** The benchmark specifically aims to address aspects of human-robot interaction (HRI) that are critical for natural and effective communication, including response time constraints and collaborative dialogue.
*   **Technical Framework: Retico:** To support the proposed benchmark and facilitate the development of spoken dialogue capabilities in robots, the authors introduce \textit{Retico}, a real-time communication framework.
*   **Future Vision:** The paper outlines a vision for extending the benchmark and framework to encompass a wider range of HRI aspects relevant to the research community.

## Methodology

The paper proposes a benchmark and a technical framework (Retico) to facilitate research in spoken dialogue for social robots. The benchmark will allow for the exploration and standardization of common dialogue artifacts. Retico is presented as a tool to meet the technical requirements for real-time spoken dialogue in robotic systems.
