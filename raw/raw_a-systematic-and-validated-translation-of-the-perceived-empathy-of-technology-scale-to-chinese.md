# A Systematic and Validated Translation of the Perceived Empathy of Technology Scale to Chinese

**Authors:** Not specified in the provided text.
**Date:** Not specified in the provided text (arXiv version 1).

## Summary

The Perceived Empathy of Technology Scale (PETS) is a crucial tool for evaluating the empathic behavior of intelligent systems, particularly relevant in human-AI interaction, especially for emotional and mental health support applications. Recognizing the importance of cultural context in the perception and expression of empathy, this research focuses on creating a validated translation of PETS into Putonghua (Mandarin) using Simplified Chinese. 

The translation process adhered to established back-translation methodologies, involving multiple independent translations, expert panel reviews, and empirical testing with a total of 400 participants from both English and Chinese samples. 

Through confirmatory factor analysis and invariance testing, the study demonstrated that the Chinese version of PETS maintains the original two-factor structure with high reliability and supports configural, metric, and scalar invariance across cultures.

Beyond structural validation, the study revealed interesting findings regarding the perception of empathic system behavior: 
*   **Empathic behavior:** Rated equivalently across both English and Chinese participants.
*   **Non-empathic behavior:** Chinese participants gave substantially higher ratings to non-empathic behavior compared to English participants, suggesting that the absence of empathy is perceived and interpreted differently across cultures.

This validated Chinese PETS version is vital for researchers and practitioners aiming to assess empathy perceptions among Chinese-speaking users, thereby supporting the development of culturally appropriate empathic technologies.
