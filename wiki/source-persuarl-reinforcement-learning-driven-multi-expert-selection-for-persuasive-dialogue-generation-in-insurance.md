# PersuaRL: Reinforcement Learning-Driven Multi-Expert Selection for Persuasive Dialogue Generation in Insurance

## Introduction
Large Language Models (LLMs) are transforming digital communication by powering conversational agents across various sectors, including customer service, sales, and insurance. While these agents excel at factual communication, they often lack the ability to engage in truly persuasive, context-sensitive dialogue. This is particularly problematic in domains like insurance, where trust and clarity are critical. This paper introduces a novel approach to enhance the persuasive capabilities of LLM-driven agents.

## Key Contributions

*   **InsureDial Dataset:** A new dataset created to capture the specific nuances of persuasive communication within motor insurance interactions.
*   **PersuaRL Framework:** A reinforcement learning (RL)-based framework designed to equip LLM-driven dialogue agents with the ability to adaptively explore, select, and coordinate strategies from multiple expert modules. This coordination is guided by the evolving dialogue context to achieve more effective persuasion.

## Findings and Evaluations

*   The proposed PersuaRL framework was evaluated using extensive automatic and human assessments on two benchmark persuasion dialogue datasets, including the newly introduced InsureDial dataset.
*   Evaluations consistently demonstrated that PersuaRL outperforms baseline approaches by generating responses that are both contextually appropriate and highly persuasive.
