# An Agentic Approach for Active Data Collection, Travel Behavior Modeling, and Weather-Sensitive Demand Prediction

**Last updated:** 2026-08-21

## Overview
This paper presents a novel three-agent workflow that integrates conversational data collection, travel behavior modeling, and weather-sensitive demand prediction. The workflow leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) for data collection and prediction, aiming to improve the efficiency and accuracy of travel behavior research.

## Key Findings

*   **Integrated Workflow:** Proposes a three-agent workflow combining conversational data collection (via chatbot), structured data processing, and behavioral prediction.
*   **Data Collection:** A chatbot-administered, image-augmented stated-preference survey collected travel mode choices from student commuters across five weather scenarios, yielding 454 observations.
*   **Predictive Modeling:** Evaluated various models including multinomial logit, logistic regression, random forest, and multiple LLM configurations (zero-shot, persona, few-shot, vision-based).
*   **LLM Performance:** 
    *   Text-only zero-shot LLMs achieved comparable accuracy to traditional machine learning models (up to 69.9%).
    *   Vision-based LLMs, incorporating weather images, achieved higher accuracy (71.5%), indicating the value of visual context.
    *   Habitual travel information consistently boosted prediction accuracy.
    *   'Expert' framing was more effective than 'Role-Play' for LLM personas.
    *   Few-shot prompting offered marginal gains after an initial small number of examples.

*   **Conclusion:** The study successfully demonstrates the coordination of conversational surveys, data processing, traditional modeling, and multimodal LLM prediction within an auditable multi-agent system.
