# Two-sided receptivity to conversational AI agents in online dating: Bilingual survey data from Fledge.Love

## Overview
This paper investigates how users of online dating platforms perceive and react to the integration of autonomous conversational AI agents and generative-AI features. The research focuses particularly on the "receiving side"—how individuals respond when encountering machine-generated profiles, messages, or conversation partners.

## Key Findings

*   **User Receptivity:** The study measures user receptivity to conversational AI agents, distinguishing between the "principal role" (deploying one's own AI agent) and the "counterpart role" (encountering another user's AI agent).
*   **Data Release:** Two anonymized bilingual (Russian and English) survey datasets were collected from active users of the Fledge.Love dating platform. The first dataset (N = 2,617) focuses on receptivity to conversational agents, while the second (N = 2,894) assesses interest in passive generative-AI features.
*   **Research Support:** The release includes model-derived scores, a bilingual codebook, a documented anonymization pipeline with a k-anonymity audit, and executable analysis notebooks. This comprehensive package aims to support further research in areas such as human-AI communication, recommender systems, and cross-cultural technology acceptance.

## Methodology

*   Surveys administered to active users of Fledge.Love.
*   Data collected in both Russian and English.
*   Analysis included receptivity batteries for conversational agents and interest measures for generative-AI features.
*   Anonymization procedures were documented and audited for k-anonymity.
