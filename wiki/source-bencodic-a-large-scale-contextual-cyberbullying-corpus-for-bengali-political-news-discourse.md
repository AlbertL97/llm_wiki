# BenCoDiC: A Large-Scale Contextual Cyberbullying Corpus for Bengali Political News Discourse

## Findings

*   **Problem:** Existing Bengali cyberbullying corpora lack contextual information, scale, and transparency, hindering robust detection and analysis of harasser behaviors.
*   **Solution:** Introduction of the BenCoDiC corpus, a large-scale (50,250 instances) resource for Bengali political news discourse, preserving caption-comment-reply relationships.
*   **Corpus Structure:** Organized using a nine-dimensional star schema covering users, videos, text, context, time, engagement, political parties, targets, and language.
*   **Annotation Process:** Initial AI chatbot evaluation failed to meet reliability criteria. A two-tier human annotation process with expert verification achieved high inter-annotator agreement ($κ=0.86$, $α=0.86$).
*   **Performance Improvement:** Transformer-based experiments show that incorporating conversational context modestly but consistently improves cyberbullying detection accuracy compared to isolated-text classification.
*   **Contribution:** BenCoDiC enables context-aware cyberbullying detection and perpetrator-oriented behavioral/network analysis in Bengali political discourse.
