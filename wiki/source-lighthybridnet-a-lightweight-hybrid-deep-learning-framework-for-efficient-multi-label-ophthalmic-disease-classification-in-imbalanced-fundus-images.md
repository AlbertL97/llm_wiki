# LightHybridNet: A Lightweight Hybrid Deep Learning Framework for Efficient Multi-Label Ophthalmic Disease Classification in Imbalanced Fundus Images

## Summary

This paper introduces LightHybridNet, a novel lightweight hybrid deep learning framework designed to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of classifying multiple ophthalmic diseases from fundus images. Addressing the challenges of subtle visual features and imbalanced datasets common in medical imaging, the framework employs advanced architectural components, pre-processing techniques, and data augmentation. Evaluated on the ODIR-5K dataset, LightHybridNet demonstrated superior performance compared to existing models, achieving high scores in weighted F1, macro AUC, and Cohen's Kappa. The study also emphasizes the importance of model interpretability, integrating explainable AI methods like Grad-CAM to identify key diagnostic features. This work signifies a substantial step towards more reliable automated ocular disease detection for clinical application.

## Key Findings

*   **Framework Development:** Introduced LightHybridNet, a lightweight hybrid deep learning framework for multi-label ophthalmic disease classification.
*   **Technical Innovations:** Integrated depthwise separable convolutions and compound scaling for improved efficiency and accuracy.
*   **Data Handling:** Utilized pre-processing (histogram equalization, color space conversion, contrast adjustment) and image augmentation to address image quality and dataset imbalance.
*   **Performance Metrics:** Achieved a weighted F1-score of 91.51%, macro AUC of 98.26%, and Cohen's Kappa of 90.14% on the ODIR-5K dataset, outperforming baseline models.
*   **Explainability:** Incorporated Grad-CAM for enhanced model interpretability, identifying features critical to diagnostic decisions.
*   **Clinical Relevance:** Positioned as a promising advancement for automated, reliable ocular disease detection in clinical settings.
