# Ensuring Safe Physical AI in Urban Mobility via Hazard-Informed Synthesized Envelopes

This paper introduces a framework for ensuring the physical safety of AI systems in urban mobility by integrating systematic hazard analysis with runtime enforcement mechanisms. It utilizes "hazard-informed safety envelopes" that transform safety across symbolic, spatial, and dynamic world models to guarantee safe operation in complex human-robot interaction scenarios.

## Key Findings

*   **Hazard-Informed Safety Envelopes:** A core contribution is the development of a unified framework that uses hazard-informed safety envelopes to ensure safety.
*   **Cross-Layer Safety Transformation:** The approach involves transforming safety information across multiple layers, including symbolic, spatial, and dynamic world models, rather than treating safety as isolated static constraints.
*   **Runtime Enforcement:** The framework is designed to interface with physical AI runtime harnesses for continuous safety enforcement.
*   **Goal of Safe Urban Mobility:** The primary objective is to guarantee safe physical interactions and mobility for AI systems in complex urban environments.
