# Gendered Anthropomorphism in Human-Robot Interaction

This article examines the influence of a robot's perceived gender on human motivation within task-oriented human-robot interactions. It delves into the concept of gendered anthropomorphism, investigating how assigning a gender to a robot can affect user engagement, perceived competence, and the overall motivation to collaborate with the robot.

## Key Findings

*   The gender assigned to a robot can significantly impact how humans perceive and interact with it in task contexts.
*   Gendered anthropomorphism plays a role in shaping human motivation and engagement during human-robot collaboration.
*   Understanding these gender dynamics is crucial for designing more effective and engaging HRI systems.
