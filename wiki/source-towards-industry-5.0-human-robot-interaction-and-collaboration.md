# Towards Industry 5.0: Human-robot interaction and collaboration

## Main Findings

*   Industry 5.0 represents a shift from the automation-focused Industry 4.0 to a more human-centric approach that prioritizes human well-being and autonomy.
*   The core of Industry 5.0 is synergistic collaboration between humans and robots, where humans contribute creativity and critical thinking, and robots provide precision and efficiency.
*   This new industrial paradigm aims to enhance resilience and sustainability within supply chains and foster ethical, safe human-robot interactions.
