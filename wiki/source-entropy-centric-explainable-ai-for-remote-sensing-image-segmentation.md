# Entropy-Centric Explainable AI for Remote Sensing Image Segmentation

## Key Findings

*   **Problem:** Deep neural networks used in critical domains like remote sensing suffer from a lack of transparency, leading to limited trust and adoption.
*   **Solution:** The paper proposes an entropy-centric Explainable AI (XAI) method for semantic image segmentation to enhance transparency.
*   **Evaluation:** A new XAI evaluation methodology is introduced to measure the relevance of highlighted regions.
*   **Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed XAI method is superior to existing methods for semantic segmentation.
