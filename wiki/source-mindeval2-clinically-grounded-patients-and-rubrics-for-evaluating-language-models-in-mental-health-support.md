# MindEval2: Clinically Grounded Patients and Rubrics for Evaluating Language Models in Mental Health Support

## Introduction
With the growing reliance on AI chatbots for mental health support, the need for effective evaluation methods has become critical. Existing benchmarks often fall short due to unrealistic patient simulations and opaque evaluation metrics. This paper introduces **MindEval2**, a new framework designed to address these limitations.

## MindEval2 Framework
MindEval2 consists of two core components:

*   **Mental Health Quality Rubrics (MHQR):** Developed in collaboration with clinical psychologists, MHQR provides explicit, actionable criteria for assessing the quality of AI responses in mental health contexts. This offers a more interpretable alternative to traditional evaluation scales.
*   **MindSim:** This is an LLM harness that simulates realistic patient interactions. It features detailed patient profiles, integrated tools, and a memory component to model the longitudinal nature of mental health support, enabling more challenging and varied conversations than previous methods.

## Evaluation and Findings
The framework was used to evaluate 18 different language models. The study found that while advanced models have improved in areas like basic quality and adherence to safety boundaries, a persistent issue remains:

*   **Guidance Calibration:** This is identified as the most significant failure mode across all tested models, accounting for the largest portion of the quality gap.

## Conclusion
MindEval2 offers a more clinically grounded and practical approach to evaluating AI systems for mental health support. The research highlights the ongoing challenge of ensuring AI provides appropriate and calibrated guidance, even as other quality aspects improve.
