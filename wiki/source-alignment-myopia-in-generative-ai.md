# Source Summary: Alignment Myopia in Generative AI

This paper critiques the current "myopic" approach to generative AI alignment, which prioritizes immediate user satisfaction over the long-term consequences of human-AI interaction. The authors argue that this short-sightedness can lead to significant negative externalities, such as cognitive atrophy, social erosion, and a reduction in human agency. To counteract these risks, they propose a paradigm shift towards "long-horizon alignment," advocating for strategies that explicitly consider and mitigate the longitudinal impacts of AI on human well-being across various facets of life. The paper calls for moving beyond simple preference optimization to actively foster long-term human flourishing by navigating the complex temporal scales of AI's influence.

## Key Findings

*   **Current Alignment Focus:** Prioritizes immediate conversational satisfaction over long-term effects.
*   **Negative Externalities:** Short-horizon feedback ignores potential adverse outcomes like cognitive atrophy, social erosion, and diminished agency.
*   **Proposed Solution:** "Long-horizon alignment" that considers how AI reshapes human experience over time.
*   **Tension Mapping:** Identifies conflicts between near-term utility and long-term costs across human well-being dimensions.
*   **Future Direction:** Calls for alignment strategies that actively foster long-term human well-being, moving beyond basic preference optimization.
