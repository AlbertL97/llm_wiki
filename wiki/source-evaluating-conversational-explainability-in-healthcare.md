# Evaluating Conversational Explainability in Healthcare: Comparing the Explainability Assistant and a Traditional XAI Dashboard

This paper evaluates the use of conversational AI for explaining medical AI models in healthcare, comparing a new "Explainability Assistant" chatbot with a traditional XAI dashboard. The study found that while the conversational approach offered easier access and patient-specific explanations, the dashboard allowed for better inspection of global and local information. Neither interface was definitively superior, leading to a recommendation for integrating both conversational and dashboard elements in clinical workflows to enhance user understanding of AI decisions.

## Key Findings

*   **Complementary Strengths:** Both conversational AI and traditional dashboards have distinct advantages in explaining medical AI, with no clear overall winner.
*   **Conversational Benefits:** Easier entry point and suitability for patient-specific explanations.
*   **Dashboard Benefits:** Superiority in inspecting multiple pieces of information (global and local) simultaneously.
*   **Integration is Key:** The optimal solution likely involves combining both conversational and dashboard interfaces.
