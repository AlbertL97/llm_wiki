# The Labor of Storytelling: How People with Chronic Illnesses Leverage AI and Communities for Narrative Translation

This article examines the role of Artificial Intelligence (AI), specifically large language models (LLMs), in assisting individuals with chronic illnesses to translate their lived experiences into narratives. These experiences, often conveyed through bodily symptoms, require articulation and influence how suffering is perceived and managed. AI is emerging as a new mediator in this process, complementing traditional community support.

## Key Findings

*   **AI's Role in Narrative Translation:** LLMs are actively participating in "narrative translation," the process of transforming personal illness experiences for particular audiences and purposes.
*   **Synergy Between AI and Communities:** Participants identified complementary roles for both AI and their established support communities in their storytelling efforts.
*   **Factors Influencing Trust in AI:** Trust in AI systems was found to be shaped by users' understanding of the AI's limitations and their perception of the AI's capacity to comprehend their experiences.
*   **Design Implications:** The insights gained from this research are intended to guide the development of future AI systems designed to provide narrative support to individuals dealing with chronic illnesses.

## Methodology

The study employed prototype-driven semi-structured interviews with 21 individuals living with chronic illnesses.
