# Source Summary: Seeing through the Eyes of AI: Situated Explainability in Augmented Reality

This paper introduces **Situated Explainability in Augmented Reality (AR)** as a novel method to enhance the understanding of Artificial Intelligence (AI) decisions. Traditional methods for explaining AI behavior, often presented on 2D displays, require users to mentally connect abstract information to the physical world. This disconnect can lead to difficulties in interpreting AI actions, especially when AI is integrated into real-world environments.

The authors propose leveraging AR to overlay AI explanations directly into the user's physical workspace in real time. This approach aims to provide **contextual relevance** and reduce cognitive load by allowing users to see explanations directly associated with the objects and locations involved in the AI's decision-making process.

Key findings and contributions:

*   **Problem:** Existing Explainable AI (XAI) methods struggle with contextual understanding in physical environments due to the separation of explanation from the real world.
*   **Solution:** Implement situated explainability using AR, which spatially integrates AI explanations into the user's view.
*   **Adaptation:** Demonstrate how existing XAI techniques can be applied within AR interfaces.
*   **User Studies:** Conducted user experiments to evaluate the effectiveness and user experience of this AR-based approach.
*   **Benefits:** Potential for improved AI comprehension, reduced cognitive burden, and enhanced user trust in AI systems operating in physical spaces.
