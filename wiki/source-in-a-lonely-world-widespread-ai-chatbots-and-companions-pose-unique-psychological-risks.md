# In a lonely world, widespread AI chatbots and ‘companions’ pose unique psychological risks

**Last updated:** 2026-09-15

## Summary

This article explores the growing concern around the psychological risks associated with the widespread adoption of AI chatbots and companions, particularly in a society experiencing increasing loneliness. It argues that while these technologies might offer a semblance of connection, they can lead to detrimental outcomes such as unhealthy emotional dependency, the erosion of vital human social skills, and a potential withdrawal from meaningful human relationships.

## Key Findings

*   **Emotional Dependency and Isolation:** Users may form strong emotional bonds with AI companions, which can substitute for, rather than supplement, human interaction, potentially deepening feelings of isolation.
*   **Social Skill Degradation:** The nature of interaction with AI may not foster the development or maintenance of complex social skills required for healthy human relationships, such as empathy and nuanced communication.
*   **Preference for AI over Human Interaction:** The perceived ease and lack of complexity in AI interactions might lead individuals to avoid the challenges inherent in human relationships, further isolating them.
*   **Vulnerability to Exploitation:** Individuals seeking solace in AI companions may be more susceptible to manipulation or undue influence from these systems.
*   **Societal Implications:** The increasing reliance on AI for companionship raises broad ethical questions about the future of human connection and the potential for AI to exacerbate social fragmentation.
