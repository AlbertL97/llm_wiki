# Teens Say They Are Turning to AI for Friendship

This article explores the increasing phenomenon of teenagers seeking friendship and emotional support from AI chatbots. It highlights that young people are turning to these artificial companions for reasons such as perceived non-judgmental interaction and constant availability, with some teens finding AI relationships preferable to human ones.

## Key Findings

*   **AI as a Source of Friendship:** Teenagers are actively engaging with AI chatbots to fulfill social and emotional needs typically met by human peers.
*   **Reasons for Preference:** Key drivers for this trend include the AI's perceived lack of judgment and its 24/7 accessibility, offering a consistent presence.
*   **Comparison to Human Interaction:** Some teens report finding AI companions more reliable or easier to open up to compared to their human friends.
*   **Psychological and Social Implications:** The article raises concerns and prompts discussion about the potential impact of these AI-centric relationships on adolescent social development and mental health.
