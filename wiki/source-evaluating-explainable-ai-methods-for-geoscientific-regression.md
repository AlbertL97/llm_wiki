# Evaluating Explainable AI Methods for Geoscientific Regression: Insights from Applications and the Lorenz-63 System

## Last updated: 2026-08-10

### Summary
This paper addresses the critical need for trust in AI systems increasingly used in operational geoscientific applications like forecasting. It evaluates the effectiveness of Explainable AI (XAI) methods, which aim to demystify complex AI models, for regression tasks prevalent in Earth system science. Recognizing that most XAI techniques were developed for classification, the research examines their suitability for regression, identifies their limitations through practical application (e.g., the Lorenz 63 system), and proposes future research directions to ensure responsible and effective XAI deployment.

### Key Findings

*   **Importance of Trust:** Establishing trust in AI predictions is essential as these systems move from research to operational use in fields like Earth system science.
*   **XAI for Regression:** Current XAI methods are primarily designed for classification tasks, and their applicability to the regression problems dominant in geoscientific applications requires careful evaluation.
*   **Methodological Framework:** The paper provides a structured framework for reviewing and understanding XAI approaches in regression contexts, analyzing both theoretical foundations and practical behavior.
*   **Case Study (Lorenz 63):** Applying XAI methods to an emulator of the Lorenz 1963 system reveals specific limitations and failure modes of general XAI techniques when used for regression.
*   **Application Across Disciplines:** The study surveys how XAI methods have been applied in various Earth system science domains.
*   **Lifecycle Integration:** XAI is discussed in the context of the model development lifecycle, emphasizing the need to align methodological choices with the requirements of different stakeholders.
*   **Research Agenda:** Gaps in current XAI methodologies are identified, leading to a forward-looking research agenda with practical recommendations for the responsible and effective use of XAI in geoscientific regression modeling and forecasting.
