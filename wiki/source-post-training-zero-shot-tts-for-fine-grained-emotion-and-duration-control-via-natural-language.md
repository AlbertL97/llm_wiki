# Source Summary: Post-Training Zero-Shot TTS for Fine-Grained Emotion and Duration Control via Natural Language

This paper introduces a post-training framework designed to enhance Text-to-Speech (TTS) systems by enabling precise, natural-language-driven control over segment-level emotion and speech duration. Addressing the limitations of existing utterance-level conditioning, the proposed method utilizes supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning to equip pretrained TTS models with the ability to generate speech that dynamically conveys a range of emotions and adapts pacing within an utterance. This approach significantly improves controllability for applications like audiobook narration and conversational agents while preserving speech intelligibility and speaker identity.

## Key Findings

*   Developed a unified post-training framework for TTS models to achieve fine-grained emotion and duration control.
*   Implemented natural language as the interface for controlling speech characteristics at a segment level.
*   Employed a combination of supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning (Group Relative Policy Optimization) for robust control.
*   Demonstrated significant improvements in controllability without compromising speech quality (intelligibility) or speaker identity.
*   Highlighted the effectiveness of post-training as a method for extending the capabilities of existing TTS architectures.
