# Source Summary: Human-Centered Explainable AI for TinyML Edge Devices: A Pareto-Based Selection Framework with LLM-Guided Design

## Overview

This paper introduces a novel framework for selecting Explainable AI (XAI) methods specifically designed for resource-constrained TinyML edge devices. The framework prioritizes a human-centered approach, integrating a Large Language Model (LLM) to guide the selection process based on qualitative stakeholder preferences. It aims to optimize the trade-offs between explanation quality (fidelity, stability) and deployment costs, making AI more understandable and trustworthy, particularly in critical applications such as healthcare.

## Key Findings

*   **Human-Centered XAI for TinyML:** The research tackles the challenge of making AI predictions on low-power edge devices interpretable and understandable to users (healthcare professionals and patients).
*   **LLM-Guided Method Selection:** A key innovation is the use of an LLM to translate abstract user needs and preferences into a set of viable XAI techniques suitable for TinyML constraints.
*   **Multi-Objective Optimization:** The framework employs Pareto-based optimization to identify the best trade-offs among multiple objectives: explanation quality (defined by metrics like fidelity and stability) and the practical cost of deploying the XAI method on the edge device.
*   **Facilitating Informed Decisions:** By providing clear insights into these trade-offs, the framework aims to support users in making more informed decisions, fostering trust in AI systems.
*   **Healthcare Application Focus:** The proposed solution is particularly relevant for clinical applications where local, explainable AI is needed for timely and reliable inference.
*   **Evaluation Methodology:** A proof-of-concept evaluation demonstrates the framework's ability to systematically compare candidate XAI methods and highlight efficient trade-offs using a skin lesion classification task.
*   **Future Work:** The authors acknowledge that physical deployment validation on hardware and empirical testing with human experts are necessary next steps.
