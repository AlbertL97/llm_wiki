# Source Summary: Towards an Extensible Benchmark for Spoken Dialogue with Social Robots

## Abstract

This paper addresses the challenges of integrating language models into real-time spoken dialogue systems for social robots. It proposes an extensible benchmark to evaluate common dialogue artifacts, such as clarification requests, interruptions, and embodied signals, and introduces the Retico framework to support these capabilities. The work aims to advance the field of human-robot interaction by providing standardized tools and methodologies for research.

## Key Findings

*   **Benchmark for Spoken Dialogue:** A new benchmark is proposed to standardize the evaluation of spoken dialogue in human-robot interaction.
*   **Focus on Interaction Artifacts:** The benchmark targets crucial elements like clarification requests, interruptions, embodied signals (e.g., head nods, facial cues), and time constraints.
*   **Retico Framework:** The paper introduces Retico, a real-time communication framework designed to enable robots to possess robust spoken dialogue capabilities.
*   **Extensibility:** The benchmark is designed to be extensible, allowing for the inclusion of other important aspects of human-robot interaction in the future.
