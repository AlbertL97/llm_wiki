# AI Companions for Young Adult Caregivers: Practical Advice, Emotional Depth, and the Limits of Metric Evaluation

**Last updated:** 2026-11-18

## Introduction
This research explores the development and evaluation of Carebot, an AI companion specifically designed to support young adult caregivers (YACs). The study highlights the challenges in creating AI that effectively balances practical assistance with emotional depth for a vulnerable user group.

## Methodology

*   The study utilized an open-weight LLM (Gemma 3-27B) as the base model for Carebot.
*   Various adaptation techniques were compared, including fine-tuning (using YAC conversations and psychologist data), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and a combination of both.
*   Well-being experts evaluated the different configurations and preferred the fine-tuned model for its perceived warmth and tailored advice.
*   An LLM judge also favored the fine-tuned model.
*   A user study involving 90 YACs was conducted, comparing the fine-tuned Carebot with the base LLM.

## Key Findings

*   **Expert vs. User Perception:** While experts and an LLM judge favored the fine-tuned Carebot for its practical advice and perceived warmth, YACs in the user study found its responses to be overly practical and brief, lacking sufficient emotional support.
*   **Base Model Contrast:** Users interacting with the base LLM perceived its responses as more emotionally oriented, though less practical.
*   **Fine-tuning Impact:** Fine-tuning, intended to enhance support, resulted in responses that were practical but not emotionally supportive for the users.
*   **Evaluation Gaps:** The study reveals a significant discrepancy between metric-based evaluations (expert and LLM) and the actual user experience and perception of the AI's helpfulness.

## Conclusion and Recommendations

*   The effectiveness of AI support tools for YACs is not adequately captured by current evaluation methods.
*   There is a critical need for situation-sensitive emotional depth in AI companions designed for caregiving contexts.
*   Future research must develop aligned evaluation frameworks that can accurately measure the emotional resonance and genuine support provided by AI to users.
