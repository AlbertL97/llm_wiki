# MOONWALK: Mediating Operations with Intent-Evidence-Action Alignment Across Junior-Supervisor Review Workflows in Animation/VFX Pre-Production

**Last updated:** 2026-09-10

## Introduction

This paper addresses the challenges of communication and clarity in animation and VFX pre-production review workflows, where the translation of creative intent from senior to junior artists often results in ambiguity, drift, and loss of reasoning. The authors propose a framework for intent-evidence-action alignment to improve this process.

## Key Findings and Contributions

*   **Intent-Evidence-Action Alignment Framework:** A design framework is introduced to structure creative processes by articulating intent in a shared record, grounding judgments in evidence, and converting decisions into clear, actionable tasks.
*   **MOONWALK System:** The framework is implemented in MOONWALK, a system that provides a shared intent record, anchoring of references and specifications, structured work-in-progress comparison, and supervisor-authorized action planning.
*   **AI's Role:** AI is utilized for administrative coordination (e.g., flagging missing context, organizing notes), with human artists retaining full creative direction.
*   **Comparative Study:** An in-studio study compared MOONWALK with a chatbot interface, finding that MOONWALK significantly improved intent alignment, decision traceability, and task executability.
*   **Human Authority:** The study underscored the importance of keeping aesthetic authority and final prioritization with human practitioners.
*   **Value Proposition:** The integrated structured workflow of MOONWALK is demonstrated to be more effective than unstructured conversational AI chatbots for these pre-production tasks.
