# Communicating Uncertainty in AI-Supported Nurse Scheduling: Expert Co-Design and Evaluation of an Explainable AI Prototype

This paper focuses on the crucial aspect of communicating uncertainty within AI-driven decision support systems, specifically in the context of nurse scheduling. Researchers developed and evaluated an Explainable AI (XAI) prototype designed to assist healthcare professionals in optimizing nurse shift assignments. The study employed a human-centered design approach, utilizing mixed methods including thematic analysis, questionnaires, and eye tracking with expert users.

## Key Findings

*   **Uncertainty Misinterpretation:** A significant finding was that healthcare professionals frequently misinterpreted the uncertainty communicated by the AI in its forecasts.
*   **Impact on Decision Confidence:** This misinterpretation of uncertainty negatively impacted users' confidence in their staffing decisions.
*   **Reception of Explanations:** While explanations of AI advice were generally viewed positively by users, they did not demonstrably improve decision confidence or increase the acceptance rate of the AI's recommendations.
*   **Design Implications:** The research provides eight concrete design implications aimed at creating more transparent and effective AI decision support tools for healthcare settings, emphasizing the need for careful consideration of how uncertainty and explanations are presented to end-users.
