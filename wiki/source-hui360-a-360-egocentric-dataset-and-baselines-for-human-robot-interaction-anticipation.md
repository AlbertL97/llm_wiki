# HUI360: A 360° Egocentric Dataset and Baselines for Human-Robot Interaction Anticipation

## Overview

This paper addresses the critical need for robots to anticipate human intentions in human-populated environments to exhibit proactive and socially aware behaviors. It introduces **HUI360**, a comprehensive dataset designed for human-robot interaction (HRI) anticipation, aiming to advance the capabilities of embodied agents in understanding spontaneous human actions.

## Key Findings & Contributions

*   **HUI360 Dataset:** The paper presents the HUI360 dataset, characterized as the largest publicly available dataset for in-the-wild HRI anticipation. It was collected from a mobile robot's egocentric viewpoint over an extended period and across various environments, capturing diverse and natural human behaviors.
*   **Annotation Pipeline:** A robust pipeline for automatic annotation of 360-degree videos is provided, including manual refinement tools. This pipeline generates detailed annotations such as 2D poses, facial keypoints, and segmentation masks, curated for high quality.
*   **Benchmark Baselines:** The research establishes benchmark baselines for interaction anticipation tasks and pioneers cross-dataset evaluations by including annotations from another mobile robot dataset (SSUP-HRI).
*   **Impact on HRI:** The dataset and baselines are intended to facilitate the development and generalization of AI models that can better predict and respond to human intentions, thereby enhancing social awareness in human-robot interactions.
