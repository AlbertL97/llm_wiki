# INDRA: A New AI Tool for Exploring Tobacco, Fossil Fuel, and Chemical Industry Archives

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12

## Introduction

The article introduces INDRA, a novel AI research platform designed to make vast, formerly secret industry archives (e.g., tobacco, fossil fuel, chemical) accessible and analyzable by Large Language Models (LLMs). Existing LLMs often suffer from hallucinations and biases when dealing with such data due to a lack of direct access to curated corpora. INDRA aims to bridge this gap by integrating archival historiography principles into its core protocol.

## Key Features and Safeguards

INDRA federates multiple industry document libraries and employs three primary safeguards:

*   **Closed Evidentiary Sandbox:** Restricts the LLM to a specified corpus, preventing external data contamination and bias.
*   **Real-time Provenance Tagging:** Clearly distinguishes between direct archival evidence and the model's inferences, enhancing transparency.
*   **System-level Protocol:** Utilizes deterministic scripts to standardize output structures, ensuring consistency and auditability.

## Objectives and Outcomes

The platform is engineered to prevent LLMs from conflating documented facts with their own generated content. This allows for massive, multi-archival investigations with outputs that are verifiable and auditable, making the knowledge production process transparent. The paper also discusses specific analytical challenges and limitations observed in case studies, such as the "Heraclitus effect" and the "gullibility problem."
