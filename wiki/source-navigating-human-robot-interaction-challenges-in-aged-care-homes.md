# Navigating Human-Robot Interaction Challenges in Private and Residential Aged Care Homes

This chapter examines the complexities of implementing human-robot interaction (HRI) in aged care settings, focusing on both residential aged care (RAC) homes and private residences. It identifies these environments as challenging due to their dynamic nature, diverse user needs, and limited technical support. The authors review various robotic and ambient intelligence systems designed to assist older adults with daily living, safety, and social engagement. Crucially, the chapter emphasizes user-centered design principles, prioritizing accessibility for individuals with low digital literacy through intuitive interfaces and straightforward interactions. It also addresses critical concerns of privacy, trust, and acceptability by proposing practical strategies such as on-device processing, data minimization, and transparent data handling. Evaluation methods are geared towards real-world effectiveness, looking at metrics like fault recovery time and staff workload, with an aim for scalable, regulation-ready solutions that enhance the dignity, safety, and independence of older adults.

## Key Findings

*   **Challenging Environments:** Aged care homes and private residences present unique HRI challenges due to cluttered layouts, dynamic conditions, varied resident needs, and limited technical support.
*   **System Applications:** Robotic and ambient intelligence systems are being developed for daily living assistance, safety monitoring, and social engagement, often using hybrid approaches with sensors.
*   **User-Centric Design for Elders:** Designs must accommodate the cognitive, physical, and social needs of older adults, prioritizing accessibility for those with low digital literacy via simple voice interfaces, minimal non-verbal cues, accessible companion interfaces, and safe object manipulation.
*   **Trust and Privacy Mechanisms:** Strategies like on-device inference, data minimization, visible consent controls, and transparent data handling are essential for building trust and ensuring privacy, aligning with AI regulations.
*   **Real-World Evaluation:** Effectiveness is measured through practical metrics such as false alarm rates, fault recovery times, impact on staff workload, and long-term reliability.
*   **Future Goals:** The research aims for scalable, regulation-compliant deployments that support older adults' dignity, safety, and independence.
