# [MM/AI] Mental Models in Human-AI Interaction: Methods and Challenges in the Generative and Agentic AI Era (Workshop)

## About

This workshop focuses on the construct of "mental models" within Human-AI Interaction (HMI). It highlights the current challenges in defining, operationalizing, and studying mental models, especially with the advent of opaque and agentic AI systems like generative AI.

## Key Findings & Discussion Points

*   **Operationalization Challenges:** The concept of mental models is often used intuitively and interchangeably with related terms like "folk theories" and "sensemaking." Diverse elicitation methods exist, each with different underlying assumptions about what constitutes a mental model.
*   **Impact of Generative and Agentic AI:** These new AI systems, often opaque and acting autonomously on behalf of users, introduce significant complexities in how users form and how researchers can study their mental models.
*   **Commensurability Issues:** The inherent challenges may hinder the ability to compare findings across different studies on people's mental models of AI.
*   **Workshop Goals:** The workshop aims to critically re-evaluate the understanding and study of mental models in HMI, promoting theoretical and methodological exchange, identifying open challenges, and charting future research directions.
*   **Call for Contributions:** Short papers are invited to reflect on the conceptual and methodological foundations of mental models in AI systems.
*   **Workshop Format:** The event includes lightning talks, hands-on exercises for elicitation, and structured discussions.
