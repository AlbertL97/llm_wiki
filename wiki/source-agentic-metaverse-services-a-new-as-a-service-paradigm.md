# Agentic Metaverse Services: A New As-a-Service Paradigm

## Introduction
This paper introduces the concept of Agentic Metaverse Services (AMServ), a new service paradigm that integrates agentic Artificial Intelligence (AI) with the metaverse. It highlights the transformative role of Generative AI (GenAI) in enhancing AI agents and proposes "Agent-as-a-Service" (AaaS) as a framework for delivering these advanced agent capabilities within virtual environments.

## Key Findings

*   **GenAI-Powered Agent Evolution:** GenAI has significantly upgraded AI agents, equipping them with advanced capabilities such as autonomous learning, multi-modal interaction, content generation, and collaborative decision-making. This represents a shift from traditional chatbots to more sophisticated "agentic AI".
*   **Agent-as-a-Service (AaaS):** The paper defines AaaS as a model where an agent's core abilities (perception, decision-making, execution, collaboration, content generation) are packaged to offer customized services to users.
*   **The Metaverse Context:** The metaverse is presented as a virtual ecosystem that integrates human life, work, creation, and entertainment, supported by next-generation digital technologies.
*   **AMServ and Meta-AaaS:** The integration of agentic services and the metaverse leads to AMServ, a new form of metaverse service designed for business processing. Meta-AaaS is proposed as the underlying approach to realize AMServ, establishing a new paradigm in service computing.
*   **Applications and Future Potential:** The work discusses the characteristics and principles of AMServ and Meta-AaaS, providing examples of their applications. It posits that these advancements hold great promise for societal and service development in the AI era.

## Research Directions

The paper concludes by identifying emerging trends and outlining future research directions for AMServ and Meta-AaaS.
