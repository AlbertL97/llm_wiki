# Understanding and Resolving Failures in Human-Robot Interaction: Literature Review and Model Development

This article presents a literature review and model development focused on understanding and resolving failures within human-robot interaction (HRI).

## Key Findings:

*   **Categorization of HRI Failures:** The research systematically categorizes various types of breakdowns that occur during interactions between humans and robots.
*   **Analysis of Failure Causes:** It delves into the underlying reasons and contributing factors that lead to these interaction failures.
*   **Development of Resolution Models:** The paper proposes a model designed to aid in the understanding, diagnosis, and resolution of HRI failures.
*   **Importance for Robot Design:** The findings are crucial for improving the design of robots to be more robust, reliable, and user-friendly, thereby enhancing the overall success of human-robot collaboration.
