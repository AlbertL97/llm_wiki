# Source Summary: Empty Commitments: When Agents Promise What Their Runtime Cannot Deliver

## Overview

This paper introduces the concept of "empty commitments" made by AI agents, specifically in the context of chatbots. An empty commitment is defined as a promise for a future action (e.g., a reminder) that the agent's current configuration or tools are incapable of fulfilling. The paper distinguishes these from broken promises, as their failure is predetermined by the agent's design rather than its execution path.

## Key Findings & Concepts

*   **Definition of Empty Commitments:** A promise an agent makes that it inherently cannot deliver due to its underlying architecture or available tools.
*   **Failure Types:** The research categorizes three primary types of failures associated with empty commitments.
*   **Anchoring Condition:** A criterion to identify promises that *could* be made real by an agent's tools, thus differentiating them from impossible promises.
*   **Outcome Taxonomy:** A classification system for the different results that occur when an agent makes an empty commitment.
*   **Measurement Protocol:** A proposed experimental framework to study empty commitments, involving follow-up requests across five setups that gradually introduce persistence capabilities, with varying degrees of environmental explicitness.

## Relevance to Wiki Concepts

*   **Trust:** Empty commitments directly impact user trust in AI agents. When an agent makes promises it cannot keep, it can lead to user frustration and erosion of confidence.
*   **Human-AI Interaction:** The paper provides insights into the communication dynamics between humans and AI, highlighting a specific failure mode that can disrupt smooth interaction.
*   **Chatbots:** The research is directly applicable to the design and evaluation of conversational agents, where making and fulfilling commitments is a crucial aspect of user experience.
*   **Measurement Tools:** The proposed measurement protocol contributes to the development of methods for evaluating AI agent behavior and its impact on users.
