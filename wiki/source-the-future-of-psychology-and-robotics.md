# The Future of Psychology and Robotics

## Key Findings

*   The fields of psychology and robotics are becoming increasingly intertwined.
*   Understanding human psychological responses to robots is crucial as they integrate into daily life.
*   Robotics has potential applications in areas like mental health support and companionship.
*   Psychological research can guide the ethical and effective development of AI and robotic systems.
*   Key areas of consideration include anthropomorphism, trust, and ethical implications of AI companions.
