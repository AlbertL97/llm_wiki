# Trusting AI in Competitive Markets

## Abstract

This paper investigates the dynamics of trust in AI recommendations within competitive market environments, specifically focusing on oligopoly pricing. It highlights that individuals' trust in AI can diverge, with some becoming more reliant and others losing faith. The study empirically examines how market gender composition influences the adoption and effectiveness of AI pricing advice. Results indicate that AI recommendations significantly increase prices and profits in female-only markets, a phenomenon attributed to learned trust and self-serving attribution. Conversely, no significant impact is observed in male-only or mixed-gender markets, challenging prior expectations about gender and AI interactions. The findings underscore the importance of considering human factors alongside algorithmic design in platform governance and regulatory oversight.

## Key Findings

*   **Divergent Trust in AI:** Users' trust in AI advice can evolve differently over time, leading to increased or decreased reliance.
*   **Market Context Matters:** The effectiveness of AI recommendations is highly dependent on the market setting, particularly the presence of AI advice and the gender composition of market participants.
*   **Gendered Impact of AI:** In a competitive pricing experiment, AI recommendations positively impacted prices and profits exclusively in female-only markets.
*   **Learned Trust and Attribution:** Analysis suggests that in female-only markets, positive outcomes reinforce adherence to AI (learned trust), while negative outcomes might be attributed to external factors (self-serving attribution).
*   **Reversal of Expectations:** The observed gendered effects diverge from some existing literature on gender and AI, suggesting a need for nuanced understanding.
*   **Human Factors in AI Adoption:** The study emphasizes that the human element, influenced by social dynamics like gender composition, plays a critical role in shaping how AI's effects manifest.

## Managerial and Regulatory Implications

*   **Holistic Approach:** Platform governance and regulations must consider both the AI algorithm and the human users who interact with it.
*   **Contextual Awareness:** Understanding socio-demographic influences, such as market gender composition, is vital for predicting AI's impact.
