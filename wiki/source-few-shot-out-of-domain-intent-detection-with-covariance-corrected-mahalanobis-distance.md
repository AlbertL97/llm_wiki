# Few-Shot Out of Domain Intent Detection with Covariance Corrected Mahalanobis Distance

This paper focuses on the critical problem of Out-of-Domain (OOD) intent detection for conversational agents like chatbots and voice assistants. The goal is to enable these agents to accurately identify when a user's utterance expresses an intent that falls outside of their training data, categorizing it as 'unknown' or 'out of domain'. The research builds upon existing work using Mahalanobis distance, which has demonstrated effectiveness, but notes its limitations in few-shot learning scenarios. 

## Key Findings:

*   **Problem:** Existing Mahalanobis distance methods for OOD intent detection perform poorly in few-shot learning settings.
*   **Analysis:** The paper analyzes the underlying reasons for this performance degradation.
*   **Solution:** A novel covariance corrected Mahalanobis distance is proposed to enhance OOD intent detection, particularly when training data is scarce.
