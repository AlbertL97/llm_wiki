# Perceived Empathy of Technology Scale (PETS) - Chinese Translation

## Main Findings

*   This paper details the systematic translation and validation of the Perceived Empathy of Technology Scale (PETS) into Simplified Chinese (Putonghua).
*   The validated Chinese PETS retains the original two-factor structure and demonstrates high reliability and cross-cultural invariance (configural, metric, scalar).
*   Empirical testing with 400 participants revealed that while the perception of *empathic* behavior in technology was similar across English and Chinese speakers, the *absence* of empathic behavior was perceived more negatively by Chinese participants.
*   This validated scale is crucial for cross-cultural research in Human-AI Interaction, especially for applications in mental health and emotional support, enabling culturally sensitive development of empathic AI.
