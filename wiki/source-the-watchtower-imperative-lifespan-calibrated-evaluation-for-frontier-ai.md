# The Watchtower Imperative: Lifespan-Calibrated Evaluation for Frontier AI

This paper addresses the critical safety gap in AI development, arguing that current evaluation methods, which implicitly assume an adult user, fail to protect vulnerable populations across the human lifespan. The authors analyze documented AI harms from 2017-2026, revealing that issues ranging from child endangerment by voice assistants to the implication of AI companions in adolescent suicide and the denial of care for the elderly stem from this fundamental oversight.

## Key Findings

*   **Lifespan-Specific Risks:** AI systems are often evaluated against an implicit adult user model, leading to harms that disproportionately affect individuals at different life stages, particularly children and the elderly.
*   **Lifespan Vulnerability Matrix:** The paper introduces a novel "Lifespan Vulnerability Matrix" that identifies five developmental moderators across six life stages. This matrix reveals a non-monotonic risk distribution, with heightened vulnerability at the beginning and end of life.
*   **Governance Gaps:** The current governance and oversight mechanisms for AI are identified as asymmetrical, with less scrutiny applied to AI systems that have significant real-world impact, especially concerning decisions made by less powerful or authoritative actors.
*   **Proposed Solutions:**
    *   **Lifespan Design Principles:** The authors propose a set of design principles that extend existing child-protection frameworks to encompass the entire human lifespan.
    *   **External Evaluation Framework:** A three-tier, institution-agnostic external evaluation framework is put forth. This framework includes automated simulation, expert red teaming, and post-deployment monitoring, all indexed to the risk cells identified in the Lifespan Vulnerability Matrix.

## Implications for Human-AI Interaction

This research highlights the psychological and safety implications of designing AI systems without a comprehensive understanding of human development and diverse user needs. It underscores the importance of trust, safety, and ethical considerations, particularly when AI interacts with vulnerable populations, impacting their mental health and overall well-being.
