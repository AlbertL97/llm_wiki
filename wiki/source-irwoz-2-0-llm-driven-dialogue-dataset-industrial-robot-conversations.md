# IRWOZ 2.0: A Large Language Model-driven Dialogue Dataset for Industrial Robot Conversations

## Introduction

The IRWOZ dataset has been instrumental in advancing dialogue systems for industrial Human-Robot Interaction (HRI). However, limitations in the original version, particularly noise in dialogue states and utterances, have hindered accurate state tracking. This paper introduces IRWOZ 2.0, a significantly improved dataset designed to overcome these challenges and facilitate more robust industrial HRI research.

## Dataset Enhancements

IRWOZ 2.0 leverages the power of Large Language Models (LLMs), including Mistral and Claude-3.5, for both data generation and quality refinement. The dataset has been expanded to encompass 390 dialogues spread across four key industrial domains: Assembly, Delivery, Position, and Relocation.

Key improvements in IRWOZ 2.0 include:

*   **LLM-driven Refinement:** Utilizes LLMs to generate and clean dialogue data.
*   **Expanded Scope:** Covers 390 dialogues across 4 industrial domains.
*   **Quality Assurance:** Features manual corrections and automated removal of typos.

## Performance Improvements

Benchmark experiments conducted on dialogue state tracking (DST) demonstrate the effectiveness of IRWOZ 2.0. By using GPT-2 as a baseline, the study shows a substantial increase in the BLEU-4 score, escalating from 0.1651 with the original IRWOZ dataset to an impressive 0.5604 with IRWOZ 2.0. This enhancement underscores the dataset's ability to support more accurate and reliable HRI dialogue systems.

## Public Release

To foster further research and development in the field of industrial HRI, the IRWOZ 2.0 dataset has been made publicly available. Researchers can access it at [IEEE Dataport](https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/irwoz-20-large-language-model-driven-dialogue-dataset-industrial-robot-conversations).
