# Beyond Large Language Models: A Mental World Model Framework for Mental Modeling, Simulation, Evolution, and Interpretability

This paper introduces a novel framework, the Mental World Model (MWM) and its implementation, the Multi-Branch Mental Evolution Architecture (MMEA), designed to advance artificial intelligence towards deeper social cognition and general intelligence. The MWM and MMEA address limitations in current Large Language Models (LLMs) and Theory of Mind (ToM) methods by explicitly constructing subjective worlds, modeling the dynamic interactions between cognition and mental states, and simulating the continuous evolution of mental states over time based on historical experiences.

## Key Findings

*   **Novel Framework:** Introduction of the Mental World Model (MWM) and the Multi-Branch Mental Evolution Architecture (MMEA).
*   **Addressing Limitations:** Overcomes current LLMs and ToM methods' deficiencies in unified mechanisms for subjective world construction, cognitive-psychological interactions, dynamic evolution, and historical dependencies.
*   **Core Functionality:** The framework explicitly constructs subjective worlds and models interactions between cognition and mental states.
*   **Simulated Evolution:** Simulates continuous mental evolution shaped by historical experiences.
*   **Performance:** Achieved superior accuracy on the DynToM (96.48%) and RecToM (94.00%) benchmarks, outperforming leading LLMs and specialized ToM methods.
*   **Significant Improvements:** Demonstrated substantial gains in mental state and behavior prediction (+28.7 pp), long-sequence prediction (+39.3 pp), and implicit mental state inference (+16.6 pp).
*   **Interpretability:** Provides an interpretable framework for continuous mental modeling.
*   **Enhanced AI Capabilities:** Enables AI to move beyond surface-level semantic representations to infer underlying mental states and dynamically simulate internal cognitive processes and their evolution.
*   **Future Potential:** Offers a foundation for causal reasoning, personalized AI, sustained human-AI interaction, and advancements towards Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).
