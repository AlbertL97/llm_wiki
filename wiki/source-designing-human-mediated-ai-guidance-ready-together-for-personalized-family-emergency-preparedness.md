# Designing Human-mediated AI Guidance: Ready Together for Personalized Family Emergency Preparedness

## Introduction
Artificial intelligence (AI) systems are increasingly used to provide personalized information and support. However, in many situations, AI-generated content requires human interpretation and adaptation before being delivered to the end-user. This paper introduces the concept of **human-mediated AI guidance**, focusing on scenarios where a knowledgeable human acts as an intermediary between an AI system and a less informed recipient.

## The 'Ready Together' System
The research explores this concept through **'Ready Together,'** an AI-supported system designed for personalized family emergency preparedness. In this system, parents act as mediators, interpreting and delivering AI-generated guidance to their children. The system aims to make emergency preparedness more interactive and understandable through family-centered learning activities.

## Design Process and Findings

*   **Qualitative Research:** The system's design was informed by qualitative methods, including semi-structured interviews and co-design activities with families.
*   **Identified Challenges:** Key challenges in family emergency preparedness included:
    *   Difficulty discussing emergencies with children.
    *   Uncertainty about providing appropriate explanations.
    *   A preference for interactive learning activities.
*   **Design Iterations:** These findings led to the development of an interactive prototype focused on guided activities and personalized recommendations.
*   **Evaluation:** Pilot studies and heuristic evaluations showed positive participant responses to the system's personalized content and practical activities.

## Core Contribution

*   **Human-Mediated AI Guidance Framework:** The paper proposes a novel framework for AI systems where humans act as essential intermediaries, adapting AI outputs to recipient needs and context.
*   **Context-Sensitive Support:** Preliminary findings suggest that this approach can effectively provide context-sensitive guidance while preserving parents' responsibility for interpreting and communicating information.
