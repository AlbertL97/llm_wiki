# Challenges in Evaluating Explanation Methods for Static and Evolving Data

This paper addresses the critical need for robust evaluation of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) methods, highlighting limitations with current approaches. The authors use the DetoxAI image recognition system for bias detection and concept unlearning as a case study to demonstrate these evaluation challenges. They emphasize the importance of human-grounded evaluations for explaining image classification methods.

Key findings and discussions include:

*   **Insufficient Evaluation:** Current XAI evaluation methods are insufficient, especially for dynamic data scenarios.
*   **Human-Grounded Evaluation:** The necessity of involving human judgment in evaluating explanations, particularly for image classification.
*   **Adapting to Evolving Data:** Exploring methods to adapt explanations when data streams evolve, including addressing concept drift.
*   **Counterfactual Explanations:** Discussing the adaptation of counterfactual explanations for evolving data streams.
*   **Co-evolution Challenges:** Identifying the difficulties in tracking the intertwined evolution of data, models, and their explanations.

This research is highly relevant to the psychology of AI/robot interaction by underscoring the importance of trustworthy and understandable AI, especially in dynamic environments. The evaluation of explanations directly impacts user trust and the effective collaboration between humans and AI systems.
