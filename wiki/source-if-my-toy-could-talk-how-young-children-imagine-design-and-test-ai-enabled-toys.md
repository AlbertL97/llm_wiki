# If My Toy Could Talk: How Young Children Imagine, Design, and Test AI-Enabled Toys

This paper investigates how young children (ages 7-9) engage with designing and testing AI-enabled toys using a technology probe called ToyTalk. Children configured AI personalities for their toys through a no-code interface and then interacted with them. The findings indicate that children predominantly designed toys with socially positive and supportive personalities. Their testing interactions focused on probing the AI's identity, knowledge, memory, and relationships. When the AI's behavior did not meet expectations, children tended to respond by correcting the AI or retesting its capabilities, rather than modifying the AI's configuration. The research discusses the implications for children's agency in designing AI, their testing strategies, and their expectations regarding the consistency of generative AI in child-focused applications.

## Key Findings

*   Children designed AI toys with socially positive personalities, supportive roles, and clear interpersonal rules.
*   The primary focus of children's testing interactions with AI toys was to probe identity, knowledge, memory, and relationships.
*   When AI behavior diverged from expectations, children typically responded through correction, persistence, and retesting.
*   Few children opted to reconfigure the AI system when discrepancies arose.
*   The study highlights the importance of children's design agency and their expectations of coherence in child-facing generative AI.

## Methodology

*   **Tool Used:** ToyTalk, a no-code technology probe.
*   **Participants:** 76 children aged 7-9.
*   **Setting:** Five elementary schools in the southeastern U.S.
*   **Procedure:** Children began with a familiar toy, configured its AI version, and then interacted with and tested the AI character.

## Implications

*   Insights into children's design agency when interacting with AI.
*   Understanding children's testing practices and expectations of AI behavior.
*   Guidance for developing coherent and child-friendly generative AI applications.
*   Potential for AI toys to influence children's perceptions of AI capabilities and social roles.
