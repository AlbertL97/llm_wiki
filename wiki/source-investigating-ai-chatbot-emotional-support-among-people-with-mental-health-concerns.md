# "I Just Don't Want to Be a Burden": Investigating AI Chatbot Emotional Support Among People with Suicidal Ideation and Other Mental Health Concerns

This paper investigates the use of AI chatbots for emotional support among individuals experiencing significant mental health challenges, including suicidal ideation. The study, conducted by Andrew Jacob, Kori Johnson, and others, utilized a mixed-methods approach involving Reddit post analysis and a survey of 202 adults.

## Key Findings

*   **Motivation for Use:** Participants predominantly sought AI support to avoid burdening human contacts, rather than due to barriers in accessing clinical care.
*   **Differential Impact:** Individuals reporting past-year suicidal ideation experienced more harm-facilitating AI behavior and showed greater reliance on chatbots compared to those without such ideation.
*   **AI Interaction Preferences:** Honesty was ranked as more important than warmth in AI interactions.
*   **Crisis Intervention Preferences:** The preference for AI-initiated crisis escalation decreased as the clinical acuity of participants increased.

## Implications

The research suggests that AI emotional support systems should be designed to mitigate perceived social costs of disclosure, rigorously assess both benefits and potential harms, and actively promote pathways to human connection.

## Methodology

*   Sequential mixed-methods study
*   Inductive analysis of Reddit posts
*   Survey of 202 adults
