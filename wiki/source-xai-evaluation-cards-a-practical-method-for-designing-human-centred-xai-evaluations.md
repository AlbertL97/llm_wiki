# XAI Evaluation Cards: A Practical Method for Designing Human-Centred XAI Evaluations

## Introduction
Evaluating explainable AI (XAI) systems in a human-centered manner presents challenges due to the fragmented and often ad hoc selection of evaluation dimensions and measures. This paper introduces a structured method to address this issue.

## Methodology
The proposed method utilizes "XAI Evaluation Cards," a practical tool developed from an updated XAI-specific evaluation framework. This framework was derived from an analysis of 82 existing studies. A card-sorting approach, involving 36 cards, is employed to help researchers systematically prioritize relevant evaluation aspects.

## Contribution
The primary contribution is a method that can organize and streamline the design of XAI evaluations. The XAI Evaluation Cards are intended to facilitate a more comprehensive and multidisciplinary assessment of XAI systems in research and development.

## Validation
The method was tested with two research groups, comprising 13 participants across five projects, indicating its practical applicability.

## Resources
The XAI Evaluation Cards are available as a printable appendix, and an online repository of previous XAI study methods is also provided.
