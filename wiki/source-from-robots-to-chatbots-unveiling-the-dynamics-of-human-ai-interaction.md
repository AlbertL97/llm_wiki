# From robots to chatbots: unveiling the dynamics of human-AI interaction

This article provides a broad overview of human-AI interaction, encompassing both traditional robots and the increasingly prevalent chatbots. It highlights the psychological dimensions of these interactions, suggesting that as AI technologies evolve, so too do the ways in which humans perceive, trust, and engage with them. The focus is on understanding the dynamics at play as AI systems become more sophisticated and integrated into everyday life.

## Key Findings:

*   The scope of human-AI interaction extends beyond physical robots to include conversational agents like chatbots.
*   Understanding the psychological dynamics of these interactions is essential due to AI's growing integration.
*   Factors influencing user perception and trust in AI include perceived intelligence and human-like conversational abilities.
