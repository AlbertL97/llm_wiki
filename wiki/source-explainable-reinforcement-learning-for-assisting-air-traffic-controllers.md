# Explainable Reinforcement Learning for assisting Air Traffic Controllers

## Introduction
The integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into high-stakes domains such as healthcare, autonomous driving, and aviation is crucial for advancing automation and fostering seamless human-AI collaboration. A fundamental prerequisite for this integration is the establishment of trust in AI-driven solutions. This trust is intrinsically linked to the explainability of AI systems, a growing area of interest, especially with the advancements in deep learning.

## Problem Statement
Despite the rapid progress in AI, significant challenges remain in building user trust, particularly when AI is applied to critical decision-making processes. This paper focuses on addressing this challenge within the domain of Air Traffic Control (ATC).

## Approach
This research investigates the application of explainability techniques to Reinforcement Learning (RL) algorithms within the safety-critical context of ATC. A simplified ATC environment serves as a testbed where an RL agent is trained to make decisions regarding alternative flight routes to avoid restricted airspace (no-fly zones).

## Explainability Method
As an initial step towards explainability, the study employs saliency maps. These maps provide visibility into which input features most significantly influence the RL agent's decision-making process, thereby offering insights into the agent's reasoning.

## Key Findings

*   **Trust and Explainability are Intertwined:** The paper emphasizes that building trust in AI, especially in critical applications like ATC, is heavily dependent on the AI's explainability.
*   **RL for ATC Decision Support:** The research demonstrates the potential of RL for assisting air traffic controllers by making informed route planning decisions.
*   **Saliency Maps Enhance Understanding:** The use of saliency maps offers a preliminary but valuable method for understanding the factors driving an RL agent's choices in a complex environment.

## Implications
This work contributes to the broader goal of developing more transparent and trustworthy AI systems for critical infrastructure. By enhancing the explainability of RL agents, this research aims to foster greater acceptance and effective collaboration between humans and AI in demanding operational settings.
