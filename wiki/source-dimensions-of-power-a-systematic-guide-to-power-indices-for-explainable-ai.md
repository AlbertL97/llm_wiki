# Dimensions of Power: A Systematic Guide to Power Indices for Explainable AI

## Introduction
This paper introduces a systematic approach to utilizing power indices for enhancing the explainability of Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems. Power indices, borrowed from cooperative game theory, are adapted to quantify the influence of various inputs on an AI's output, thereby aiding in the attribution of results and fostering explainability.

## Core Concepts and Findings

*   **Power Indices for XAI:** The primary goal is to adapt power indices to measure the contribution of individual inputs or sets of inputs to an AI model's output. This is crucial for understanding how AI systems arrive at their decisions.
*   **Three Attribution Dimensions:** The authors categorize power indices based on three key dimensions:
    *   **Single-player:** Focuses on the power of individual inputs.
    *   **Set-based:** Considers the power of specific groups or sets of inputs.
    *   **Cardinality-based:** Examines power based on the number of players (inputs) rather than their specific identities.
*   **Systematic Review and Analysis:** The paper provides a comprehensive review of existing power indices within these dimensions. It generalizes existing methods and analyzes their formal properties and principles.
*   **Theoretical Contributions:** New proofs are presented for properties of power indices that were previously absent in the literature.
*   **Impact of Choice:** The study highlights how the selection of a specific dimension and index can significantly alter the resulting attributions of AI outputs to inputs.
*   **Practical Guidance:** The paper offers concrete examples and guidance to help practitioners choose the most appropriate power index for their specific AI application context.

## Relevance to Human-AI Interaction

While the paper's primary focus is on the technical aspects of Explainable AI (XAI) and game theory, its findings have indirect but significant implications for Human-AI interaction. By providing methods to understand and quantify the influence of different inputs on AI outputs, power indices can:

*   **Enhance Trust:** A clearer understanding of AI decision-making processes, facilitated by explainability, can lead to increased user trust in AI systems.
*   **Inform Interaction Design:** Understanding which factors most influence an AI's behavior can help in designing more intuitive and predictable interactions.
*   **Support Anthropomorphism Debates:** Quantifying influence might shed light on the 'reasoning' or 'contribution' of AI components, which can be relevant to discussions about anthropomorphism and perceived agency.
