# Your psychological needs predict whether you will accept an AI companion or therapist

This study explores how individual psychological needs influence the acceptance of AI companions and therapists. The research found that greater satisfaction in psychological needs, particularly relatedness and competence, correlates with a higher likelihood of accepting AI support. Conversely, a strong need for autonomy can lead to increased skepticism towards AI, especially in therapeutic settings. These findings emphasize the importance of tailoring AI designs to individual psychological profiles to foster acceptance and effective interaction.

## Key Findings

*   Individual psychological needs significantly predict acceptance of AI companions and therapists.
*   Higher levels of relatedness and competence needs are associated with greater willingness to accept AI support.
*   A strong need for autonomy may lead to reluctance in accepting AI, particularly for therapeutic purposes.
*   Understanding psychological needs is crucial for designing acceptable and effective AI systems for social and therapeutic roles.
