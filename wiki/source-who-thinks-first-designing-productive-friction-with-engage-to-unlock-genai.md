# Who Thinks First? Designing Productive Friction with Engage-to-Unlock GenAI

## Introduction
Generative AI (GenAI) tools can be highly effective in assisting with tasks like writing. However, the ease with which users can access these tools might lead to cognitive offloading, where users delegate cognitive effort to the AI before developing their own ideas. This paper introduces a design mechanism called "Engage-to-Unlock" to mitigate this issue by introducing "productive friction."

## The Engage-to-Unlock Mechanism
Engage-to-Unlock is a design strategy that delays full access to generative AI capabilities until the user has demonstrated meaningful engagement with the task. This aims to encourage initial human contribution and prevent users from immediately offloading cognitive effort to the AI.

## Experimental Study
A controlled experiment involving 398 participants was conducted to test the Engage-to-Unlock mechanism against three other conditions: Human-Only, Standard Chatbot (frictionless access), and Time-Matched Unlock (AI unlocked at the same time point as Engage-to-Unlock but not based on user engagement). Participants performed a writing task followed by an evaluation of passages for errors.

## Key Findings

*   **Shift in Effort:** Participants in the Engage-to-Unlock condition allocated more time to the writing phase and less to the evaluation phase.
*   **Task Duration:** Overall task duration was not negatively impacted by the Engage-to-Unlock mechanism.
*   **AI Interaction:** Users in the Engage-to-Unlock group submitted more prompts to the AI compared to other AI-assisted conditions, indicating sustained engagement with the tool.
*   **Evaluation Efficiency:** The Engage-to-Unlock approach led to the highest accuracy-per-time efficiency during the evaluation of passages.

## Conclusion
The study concludes that designing GenAI access with a degree of friction, such as Engage-to-Unlock, can foster more productive human-AI interaction. This approach encourages early user engagement, preserves active AI use, and enhances the efficiency of subsequent tasks.
