# GenAI-DBT: Evaluating a DBT-Informed Generative AI Platform for Mindfulness Skills Training: A Pilot Randomized Study

This study evaluated a generative AI platform designed for Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) mindfulness skills training, comparing it against a general chatbot and a static web resource in a 30-day pilot randomized study with 45 university students. The DBT-informed AI platform (GenAI-DBT) showed promising results in terms of feasibility and acceptability, exhibiting higher adherence rates and a greater positive impact on linguistic valence compared to the general chatbot. While clinical outcomes like mindfulness and emotion regulation scores did not significantly differ between groups, the qualitative feedback highlighted the value of contextualized AI practice. The study concludes that conversational AI is a viable tool for mental health skills training, warranting further investigation in larger clinical trials.

## Key Findings:

*   **Feasibility & Acceptability:** The DBT-informed generative AI platform (GenAI-DBT) was found to be feasible and acceptable for mindfulness skills training.
*   **Adherence:** GenAI-DBT demonstrated higher participant-week adherence (71.4%) compared to a general chatbot (42.3%) and a static web resource (53.3%).
*   **Linguistic Valence:** The AI platform led to a significantly larger positive change in linguistic valence than the general chatbot.
*   **Clinical Outcomes:** No significant differences were observed in mindfulness (MAAS) or emotion regulation (DERS) scores, or in session-level mood changes, across the study conditions.
*   **Qualitative Feedback:** Participants valued scenario-based practice but identified issues with response latency, repetition, and personalization as areas for improvement.
