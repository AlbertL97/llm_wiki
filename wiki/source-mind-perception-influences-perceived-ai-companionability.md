# Mind Perception Influences Perceived AI Companionability

This wiki page summarizes the findings of the research paper "Mind Perception Influences Perceived AI Companionability" by Ethan A. D. Morrison and Nicholas Epley.

## Main Findings

*   **Mind Perception Shapes AI Companionability:** The study demonstrates that human beliefs about an AI companion's "mind" (mind perception or MP) significantly influence how companionable the AI is perceived to be.
*   **Pathways to Companionability:** Two primary ways humans perceive AI companionability emerge:
    *   **Agentic Pathway:** Perceiving the AI as an agent or resource (related to "eudaimonic exchange" or self-elevating links) is predicted by agentic MP.
    *   **Experiential Pathway:** Perceiving the AI as an attuning experiencer (related to "connective coordination" or dyadic co-presence) is predicted by experiential MP.
*   **Impact of Priming:** Describing an AI companion with mind-attributive language can reduce skepticism, particularly for connective coordination, but not eudaimonic exchange.
*   **Meaning in Life as a Moderator:** An individual's sense of meaning in life, especially existential purpose, can amplify the link between perceived AI agency and companionability.
*   **Flourishing Individuals:** Both pathways to AI companionability are more accessible to individuals who are already flourishing or have a strong sense of personal purpose.
