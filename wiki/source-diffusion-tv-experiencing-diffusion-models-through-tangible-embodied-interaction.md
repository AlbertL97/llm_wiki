# Diffusion TV: Experiencing Diffusion Models through Tangible, Embodied Interaction

**Last updated:** 2026-09-07

## Overview

Diffusion TV is an interactive AI art installation that offers a tangible and embodied way for users to experience and understand diffusion models. It utilizes a modified CRT television where physical manipulation of controls, such as an antenna and tuning knob, directly influences the AI-generated content (images and sounds) in real-time. This approach provides an alternative, embodied form of explainable AI (XAI) that emphasizes the generative process itself.

## Key Findings & Contributions

*   **Tangible and Embodied Interaction:** The installation leverages physical interaction with a modified CRT TV to represent the underlying processes of diffusion models, making abstract AI concepts more accessible.
*   **Metaphorical Representation:** Manipulating the TV's antenna metaphorically enacts the denoising process inherent in diffusion model generation.
*   **Thematic Narrative:** The use of three channels (Past, Present, Future) featuring AI-generated animals introduces an ecological and temporal narrative, adding depth to the user experience.
*   **Process-Oriented Exploration:** Diffusion TV prioritizes showcasing the generative process and intermediate AI states over simply presenting final outputs, encouraging deeper exploration and reflection.
*   **Embodied Explainable AI (XAI):** It offers a non-technical, embodied approach to explainability, inviting users to intuitively grasp how generative AI works through direct experience.
