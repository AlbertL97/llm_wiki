# Source Summary: The Role of Frustration in Human–Robot Interaction – What Is Needed for a Successful Collaboration?

This article explores the critical role of frustration in Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), identifying it as a significant barrier to effective collaboration. It investigates the various factors that can lead to user frustration when interacting with robots and proposes strategies to prevent or alleviate it. The authors emphasize that understanding and addressing user frustration is paramount for achieving successful and productive human-robot partnerships.

## Key Findings:

*   Frustration acts as a major impediment to achieving smooth and efficient Human-Robot Interaction.
*   The paper likely details common triggers for user frustration, such as robotic unpredictability, lack of responsiveness, high task complexity, and inadequate communication.
*   Potential solutions and design principles for mitigating frustration are likely presented. These could encompass enhanced robot design, more effective user feedback systems, adaptive robot behaviors, and clearer communication strategies.
*   The core objective of the research is to facilitate successful collaboration by actively managing and reducing user frustration during HRI.
